## At a glance

Zolpidem is a hypnotic used for short-term treatment of certain severe sleep problems. A review should consider the underlying sleep problem and risks from continued use. [HSE guide](https://www2.hse.ie/medicines/zolpidem/)

## The Science Behind the Drug

### The GABA-A receptor and the difference between sleep and next-day function

Zolpidem binds a regulatory site on certain GABA-A receptors and enhances inhibitory signalling. It promotes sleep but can also affect alertness, coordination and memory. Complex sleep behaviours can occur. Exposure and the timing of other sedatives can change next-day impairment. [DailyMed source](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=907c7b18-2638-512c-e053-2995a90ae6fd)

## What the evidence measures

Time to fall asleep, time awake overnight and daytime function are different outcomes. A sleep diary that includes alertness and other sedatives gives a clearer picture than reporting hours slept alone.

## Keep a sleep and alertness diary

| What to check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Night | record sleep timing, awakenings and any unusual behaviour reported by others |
| Day | note alertness, balance or memory problems |


## What if somebody tells me I did something I cannot remember?

Report unusual sleep-related behaviour to the prescriber promptly and tell them about alcohol or other sedatives. Ask for clear advice about taking further doses.

## When to ask for help or a medication review

Discuss unusual sleep behaviours promptly with a clinician. Alcohol, opioids or other sedatives can add to the risk. Difficulty waking or abnormal breathing needs emergency help. [HSE patient guidance](https://www2.hse.ie/medicines/zolpidem/)
