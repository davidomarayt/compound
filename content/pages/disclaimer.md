Rules change, thresholds move with each Budget, and your circumstances are your own. Before you act on anything involving money, check the linked source and, where the sums matter, a qualified accountant, tax adviser or financial adviser.

**Health.** Health articles describe what published research and official guidance say, including how strong or weak that evidence is. They are general information, not a diagnosis or a treatment plan. Do not start, stop or change any medication or treatment on the strength of an article. If you are unwell, worried about a symptom, pregnant, or caring for a child, talk to your GP or pharmacist. In an emergency call 112 or 999.
