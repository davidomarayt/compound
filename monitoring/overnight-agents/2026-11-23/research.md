The statistics describe **calendar-2022 welfare receipt for pupils attending in academic year 2022–2023**; they are **not current 2026 benefit eligibility, 2026 take-up or causal measures of school quality**.

| CSO indicator (2022 records) | Published measure | Calculation or caveat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pupils whose families benefited from Back to School Clothing and Footwear Allowance | **25.7%** | Percent of linked pupils, **not 2026 take-up** or annual average payout |
| Primary DEIS pupils living in disadvantaged/very disadvantaged/extremely disadvantaged areas | **32.3%** | Compared with **5.8%** of pupils attending non-DEIS primary schools: **32.3 − 5.8 = 26.5 percentage points**; **32.3 / 5.8 = 5.57x** |
| Post-primary DEIS pupils living in those deprived area categories | **28.3%** | Compared with **8.2%** for non-DEIS post-primary schools: **28.3 − 8.2 = 20.1 percentage points**; **28.3 / 8.2 = 3.45x** |
| Pupils in one-parent families | **17.6%** | **82.4%** in two-parent families by the linked administrative classification; not a causal educational-outcomes estimate |

**Why the arithmetic matters:** both ratios compare the share of **pupils within each school category** who live in certain deprivation-area categories, not the relative number of disadvantaged children in each group or the proportion of disadvantaged pupils served by DEIS. The figures cannot be used to rank individual schools, infer benefit causation, diagnose children or identify families.

**Methodology/uncertainty:** This is a CSO **Frontier Series Output**, based on pseudonymised linked Department of Education and Youth, Department of Social Protection and Child Benefit administrative records. The CSO explicitly warns that new methods/coverage may be incomplete and that welfare receipt means **at least one payment during 2022**, regardless of duration or amount. The publication date **19 Nov 2026** must not be confused with reference year **2022**. Irish benefits/payments and school classifications can change between years. See [CSO background notes](https://www.cso.ie/en/releasesandpublications/fp/fp-sepspad/socio-economicprofilesofschoolpupilsusingadministrativedata2022/backgroundnotes/).
