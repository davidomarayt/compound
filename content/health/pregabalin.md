## At a glance

Pregabalin is used for particular indications including some nerve pain, epilepsy and anxiety disorders. Benefit cannot be judged the same way across those conditions. [HSE guide](https://www2.hse.ie/medicines/pregabalin/)

## The Science Behind the Drug

### Calcium channel subunits and nerve signalling

Pregabalin binds the alpha-2-delta subunit of voltage-gated calcium channels. This can reduce release of some signalling chemicals from highly active nerve cells. Although the name and structure are related to GABA, the medicine is not a direct GABA receptor agonist. Kidney function affects clearance. [EMA source](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/lyrica)

## What the evidence measures

For pain, consider sleep and physical function as well as pain intensity. For epilepsy, seizure frequency and severity matter. The care team may review both potential benefit and sedation or balance problems.

## Keep a useful side-effect record

| What to check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Timing | note when dizziness or drowsiness occurs |
| Context | list any opioid, sleep, anxiety or other sedating medicine |


## Why does a clinician ask about kidney function?

Clearance depends substantially on the kidneys, which can affect exposure. It is part of the prescription review alongside the condition treated and any sedating co-medications.

## When to ask for help or a medication review

Combining with other sedatives can increase breathing risk. Ask about a gradual stopping plan if needed. Severe breathing difficulty or unresponsiveness requires emergency help. [HSE patient guidance](https://www2.hse.ie/medicines/pregabalin/)
