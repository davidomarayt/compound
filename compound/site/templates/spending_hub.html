04 / TRANSPARENT SOURCES

What these figures mean — and don't.

2026 estimates

Gross voted and non-voted expenditure as published for 2026. These are estimates rather than fully audited 2026 actual spending figures.

Budget 2027

Proposed spending in the Budget 2027 dataset. They must not be described as money already spent in 2027.

Different accounting boundaries

Housing was previously grouped with Additional Departments, and transfers of responsibilities can disrupt historical sector comparisons.
