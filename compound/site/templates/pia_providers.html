How this comparison is compiled

We include providers with a directly attributable public statement about Ireland's planned scheme. All entries have a link to the organisation's own published information and are listed alphabetically. We do not rank or recommend providers while fees and product documents are unavailable. Changes must be verified against an official provider source before the tracker is updated.

This directory currently contains no paid placements or affiliate referral links. If sponsorship or affiliate relationships are added, they will be visibly labelled and editorial criteria will remain independent. Investment values can fall and returns are not guaranteed.
