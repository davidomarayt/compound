## What this calculator is for

Deposit Interest Retention Tax is the tax deducted from interest on many deposit accounts held by Irish-resident individuals. This calculator separates gross interest, DIRT and net interest.

## How to read the result

DIRT applies to the **interest earned**, not the deposit principal. A €50,000 deposit does not create a €16,500 tax bill; only the interest paid is taxed at the applicable rate.

## Worked example

If a deposit earns €1,000 gross interest, 33% DIRT is €330 and the net interest is €670, assuming the standard rate applies and no exemption/refund applies.

## Irish context

Revenue states that the current DIRT rate is 33% for Irish-resident individuals. Certain people can qualify for DIRT exemption or refunds, including under specified age/disability and income conditions.

## What can change the answer

Gross interest, compounding frequency, account type, residency and exemption status can affect the tax actually deducted. Foreign deposit interest can have separate reporting requirements.

## Common mistakes to avoid

Do not apply DIRT to the full account balance. Do not assume all savings products are taxed as deposit interest. And do not compare a deposit's gross rate with an investment's after-tax return without putting both on the same basis.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the DIRT rate?
Revenue currently states 33%.

### Is DIRT charged on my deposit?
No, on the interest.

### Can anyone be exempt?
Certain qualifying individuals can be exempt or claim refunds under Revenue rules.

### Does DIRT cover shares or funds?
No. Those can fall under different tax regimes.

## Method and limitations

The tool multiplies gross deposit interest by the implemented DIRT rate and subtracts that tax from gross interest. It does not determine exemption eligibility or foreign-account reporting.
