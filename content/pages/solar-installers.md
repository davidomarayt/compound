<section class="sol-article-grid"><article><h2>What to ask each company</h2><ol><li>Is your company currently registered under the relevant SEAI scheme?</li><li>Can you show references for similar roofs and electrical installations?</li><li>What panel, inverter and battery make and exact model are you supplying?</li><li>What is the annual kWh prediction, and what assumptions support it?</li><li>What is included in writing: scaffold, access, commissioning and BER?</li><li>Which warranties cover panels, inverter, labour and roof penetration?</li></ol></article><article><h2>Warning signs</h2><p>Be cautious with high-pressure deadlines, savings guarantees without modelling, claims that approval can be obtained after work starts, refusal to supply a contract, unusually large deposits without safeguards, and unclear after-sales service.</p><p>SEAI says it does not approve, guarantee or warranty companies or their work.
