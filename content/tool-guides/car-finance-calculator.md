## What this calculator is for

This calculator compares three common ways to fund a car: a personal loan, Hire Purchase and a Personal Contract Plan. It is designed to show why the lowest monthly payment is not necessarily the lowest total cost.

## How to read the result

The three structures are shown explicitly as **Personal car loan**, **Hire Purchase**, and **Personal Contract Plan (PCP)** so that ownership timing and end-of-term obligations are not hidden by the monthly payment.

Compare **monthly payment**, **total paid**, **ownership point** and, for PCP, the final balloon/GMFV. In advanced mode, include fees, estimated end value, mileage limits and return charges.

## Worked example

A PCP can show a much lower monthly payment than HP because a large part of the car's price is deferred into the final balloon. The lower payment did not make that amount disappear; it moved it to the end of the agreement if you want to own the car.

## Irish context

CCPC explains that under HP the finance company remains the legal owner until the agreement is completed. PCP is similar but leaves a significant balloon payment and usually includes mileage/condition terms. With a personal loan, you generally own the car from the start.

## What can change the answer

APR, deposit, term, fees, balloon amount, trade-in value and likely resale value all matter. PCP economics are particularly sensitive to whether the car's market value at the end exceeds or falls below the guaranteed/final amount.

## Common mistakes to avoid

Do not compare monthly payments without equalising term and deposit. Do not assume PCP equity will exist at the end. And include excess mileage or condition charges when a return scenario is realistic.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I own the car on HP immediately?
No. The finance company is the legal owner until completion.

### Do I own the car on PCP?
Only if you exercise the keep option and pay the final balloon and other required amounts.

### Is a personal loan always cheaper?
No. Compare actual APR, fees and term.

### Is GMFV guaranteed resale value?
It is a contractual final-value concept subject to agreement conditions, not a guarantee that every market-sale scenario produces equity.

## Method and limitations

The tool amortises the loan and HP balances and uses a balloon-payment formula for PCP. Advanced mode adds the entered fees, mileage/condition charges and estimated end value. It does not model tax, insurance, depreciation curves or every contract clause.
