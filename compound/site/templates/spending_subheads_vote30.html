06 / WHY THE NUMBERS RECONCILE

Gross allocations minus receipts equals the net Vote.

The agriculture department's Vote is not simply the sum of Government support payments. Its gross programmes are partly financed by Appropriations-in-Aid, which reduce the supply requested from the Exchequer.

07 / SOURCE AND LIMITATIONS

Financial definitions

2025 is the Supplementary Estimate; 2026 is the Revised Estimate. The comparison is not a measure of final spending or service outcomes.

Coverage

Programme D has a published total but its subheads are not present in the selected committee tables. We have not fabricated values or redistributed its total.

Correct aggregation

Programmes A to D sum to gross; Appropriations-in-Aid reduce gross to the net grant. Parent totals must not be added to their child subheads again.
