## 4. Content Freshness — narrow source-backed broker protection clarification

**The original problem:** the live broker guide explained only the ICS maximum, but readers comparing a brokerage app with a bank savings account were not explicitly warned that `€20,000` investment-firm compensation and `€100,000` eligible deposit protection cover different situations. It also lacked a clear question to ask about the legal status of unused broker cash.

**Primary consumer/regulatory sources (checked 14 November 2026; first-publication dates not supplied in fetched pages):**

1. [Central Bank of Ireland: compensation schemes for consumers of authorised firms](https://www.centralbank.ie/consumer-hub/explainers/what-compensation-schemes-protect-consumers-of-authorised-firms): Irish ICS when a covered investment firm cannot return client assets; **90% of eligible net loss up to €20,000**, not an investment performance guarantee. Explicit scheme limitations and adjudication when two schemes could apply.
2. [Central Bank of Ireland: Deposit Guarantee Scheme](https://www.centralbank.ie/consumer-hub/deposit-guarantee-scheme): **eligible deposits** at covered institutions, to **€100,000 per person per institution**. Not a blanket guarantee of stocks/ETFs.
3. [Competition and Consumer Protection Commission: Investor Compensation Scheme](https://www.ccpc.ie/manage-your-money/saving-and-investments/types-of-investment/ics-protection): eligibility depends on firm, client/product and circumstances; mere authorisation is not automatic scheme eligibility; no compensation for market declines.
