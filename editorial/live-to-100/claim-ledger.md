## Decisions and exclusions

- Eurostat’s underlying 2024 dataset was accessed directly. Earlier CSO tables using 2022 figures were checked but not used for the main life-expectancy callout.
- Department of Health Key Trends 2025 pages and the general gov.ie pension page returned access errors in this environment. No claim relies on their search snippets. The life-expectancy claim uses Eurostat directly; the pension claim uses the Department’s readable MyWelfare service page.
- No population projection, survival calculator, future technology prediction, clinical treatment recommendation or detailed financial model was needed for this gateway.
- Existing internal destinations were verified against the current source tree and local build. They support further reading, not independent verification of the claims above.
