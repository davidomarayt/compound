Pin your home

Zoom in and tap to move the pin. The compass and daylight times follow the selected location. The gold line points towards the sun. Zoom in to see the estimated shadow of your chosen object.

How to read the sky

North is at the top. The outer circle is the horizon and the centre is directly overhead. The gold curve follows the selected day. Switch between June and December at the same clock time to compare where the sun sits relative to a window or garden.

Shadow length assumes level ground and a vertical object. Trees, buildings, cloud and sloping ground affect the light you actually receive. For panels, continue to the roof direction calculator, then estimate savings with the solar payback calculator.

From rent to a monthly budget

Tap the map to move the workplace. Coloured corridors follow the roads that fit your time allowance. Set a monthly ceiling to show areas with a published rent within budget. Select an area from the results for its rent and estimated drive time to the closest sampled road point. The rent applies to the whole electoral area. Individual homes and rooms can differ substantially.

Use the full Ireland rent map to compare neighbouring areas. Add your return journey and days on site to the fuel cost calculator. Students can compare commuting with renting in the college cost calculator.

How journey times and rents are estimated

The model follows connected roads in Tailte Éireann’s generalised 1:250,000 network, using your selected average speed in both directions. It includes straight-line access from your pin to the closest sampled road at 20 km/h. Many estate roads and city streets are omitted. The narrow coloured bands indicate the vicinity of reachable roads. Times exclude congestion, junction delays, parking, turn restrictions and the final journey to a particular home. A grey band means the rent was withheld.

Prices are standardised monthly rents from the RTB/ESRI Q1 2026 Rent Index. Road geometry adapted from Tailte Éireann, Roads – 250k Map of Ireland. Boundaries and roads © Tailte Éireann, CC BY 4.0.
