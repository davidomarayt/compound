Do not read the highest number as “best”. Pension tax relief may be restricted or partially available; a PIA can be accessed earlier under the proposed design. Your PIA tax uses estimated daily closing values, while the retirement-tax deduction is only a sensitivity test.

This calculator runs in your browser. Inputs are not stored, saved to a link, or sent to Google Analytics. No personalised recommendation is made.

Method: Contributions are modelled at 12 evenly spaced month-ends. Each full year uses 365 simulated closing values, smooth returns and daily percentage-fee deductions. The proposed PIA tax is 1% of the excess of the average closing value over €50,000, deducted at each modelled year end. No opening balance, withdrawals, inflation, fund-specific dealing fees or partial first tax year are modelled.

Check the source rules: Revenue pension relief limits, Revenue pension taxation, and Compound's source-linked PIA daily valuation explainer. PIA legislation and provider terms may change.
