**Semaglutide is the active ingredient in Ozempic, Wegovy and Rybelsus.** It activates a receptor used by GLP-1, a hormone involved in appetite and blood glucose regulation. Each product has its own formulation, indication and instructions. This guide covers the main adult EU uses and links to separate coverage of Irish access and costs. [EMA: Ozempic](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/ozempic) · [EMA: Wegovy](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/wegovy) · [EMA: Rybelsus](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/rybelsus)

## The Science Behind the Drug

When you eat, the gut releases **glucagon-like peptide-1**, or GLP-1. Semaglutide is a **GLP-1 receptor agonist**, meaning it attaches to that receptor and activates it. The same receptor is involved in several processes, so the effects depend on where the signal is received. [EMA product information, section 5.1](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/wegovy-epar-product-information_en.pdf)

- **Pancreas:** When blood glucose is elevated, semaglutide helps insulin release and can reduce the release of glucagon, a hormone that raises blood glucose.
- **Brain:** GLP-1 signalling in areas involved in appetite can increase fullness and reduce hunger and food intake.
- **Stomach:** Semaglutide can slow stomach emptying. The product information describes this alongside the effects on appetite and pancreatic hormones. [EMA product information, section 5.1](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/wegovy-epar-product-information_en.pdf)

## Ozempic, Wegovy and Rybelsus compared

| Product | Form in EMA product information | Main authorised adult use |
|---|---|---|
| Ozempic | Weekly injection | Type 2 diabetes |
| Wegovy | Weekly injection or daily tablet, depending on the formulation | Weight management in eligible adults |
| Rybelsus | Daily tablet | Type 2 diabetes |

The EMA documents describe the EU authorisation. Local stock and reimbursement are separate questions. Use the exact product on your prescription and check with your prescriber before switching between products. [EMA: Ozempic](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/ozempic) · [EMA: Wegovy](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/wegovy-epar-product-information_en.pdf) · [EMA: Rybelsus](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/rybelsus)

## What should you discuss with your prescriber?

Nausea, diarrhoea, vomiting and constipation can occur. Seek urgent medical advice for severe, persistent abdominal pain, which can be a sign of pancreatitis. Tell your care team if you take insulin or a sulfonylurea, are planning pregnancy, or are due to have an operation with general anaesthesia or deep sedation. The product leaflet covers these situations and other precautions in more detail. [EMA Wegovy prescribing information and leaflet](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/wegovy-epar-product-information_en.pdf)
