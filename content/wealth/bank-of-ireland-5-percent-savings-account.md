Bank of Ireland has introduced a regular savings account offering 5% annual equivalent rate (AER) on balances up to €5,000. Available since 17 November and announced on 24 November 2026, KickstartSaver is intended for people building savings from scratch. The headline rate is attractive, but the amount you can deposit is restricted.

## How does KickstartSaver work?

| Feature | Terms |
|---|---|
| Balance up to €5,000 | 5% variable AER |
| Balance above €5,000 | Advertised 2% variable rate; check the applicable balance tier |
| Minimum regular contribution | €5 |
| Weekly maximum | €58 |
| Fortnightly maximum | €115 |
| Monthly maximum | €250 |
| Minimum age | 16 |
| Access | On demand |
| Lump sums | Not accepted |

Contributions are made by direct debit. The account can be opened through the bank's mobile app or in a branch. The interest rate is variable, so the bank can change it.

## How much could you earn in the first year?

Saving €250 every month produces €3,000 in contributions after 12 months. You do not earn 5% on the entire €3,000 for the whole year because the money arrives gradually.

| Monthly saving | Annual contributions | Approximate first-year gross interest | After 33% DIRT |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| €50 | €600 | €16 | €11 |
| €100 | €1,200 | €33 | €22 |
| €150 | €1,800 | €49 | €33 |
| €250 | €3,000 | €81 | €54 |

Illustrations use a constant 5% annual rate and a simple monthly balance approximation with contributions at the start of each month. Actual interest depends on payment dates, interest calculation conventions, withdrawals and rate changes. Figures are rounded and exclude any applicable PRSI.

At €250 per month, it takes 20 contributions to reach €5,000 in principal, excluding interest. Once a balance of €5,000 has been accumulated, a full year at an unchanged 5% would generate €250 gross interest, or €167.50 after 33% DIRT. That is an illustration of the return on an existing balance, not an achievable opening deposit.

## How does DIRT affect the 5% rate?

Revenue states that the standard Deposit Interest Retention Tax rate is 33%. For every €100 of taxable interest, €33 is deducted, leaving €67. On a balance held for a full year, this makes a 5% gross rate equivalent to 3.35% after DIRT alone. Exemptions and additional PRSI liabilities can affect individual outcomes. USC does not apply to deposit interest.

## KickstartSaver versus SuperSaver

| Bank of Ireland product | Advertised rate | Best suited to |
|---|---|---|
| KickstartSaver | 5% variable up to €5,000 | Building a modest cash reserve |
| SuperSaver | 3% fixed for the first 12 months | Larger regular contributions |
| Advantage 12-month fixed term | 2.40% fixed | Existing lump sums |
| Instant Access Demand | 0.10% variable | General cash access |

Rates checked on 25 November 2026. SuperSaver permits monthly contributions up to €2,500 and has different rates after the introductory year. The fixed-term account has a €5,000 minimum deposit and limited early access. These products are not directly interchangeable.

For someone saving a small portion of every payday, KickstartSaver offers a higher initial rate than the bank's other listed regular savings products. For someone already holding substantial cash, deposit restrictions and the lower rate applicable above the threshold make a separate savings account worth comparing. Check the bank's terms for the precise treatment of balances over €5,000 before relying on a return estimate.

## Is the account worth considering?

The combination of accessible savings and regular automatic contributions may suit an emergency fund, a holiday budget or other near-term goals. The main drawbacks are the contribution cap and the possibility of future rate reductions. A high headline rate is less useful if it applies to only a small share of your total cash.

Compare other providers' current rates, access restrictions, deposit limits and applicable deposit protection before choosing an account. Cash savings and long-term investments serve different purposes; money needed soon should not be exposed to unnecessary market risk.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I deposit €5,000 immediately?

No. The product does not accept lump-sum deposits; savings are built through scheduled direct debits.

### Is the 5% rate guaranteed?

No. It is variable and may change.

### Can I withdraw my savings?

The bank advertises on-demand access. Read the current account terms for the withdrawal process.

### Can I open an account if I am 16?

The published minimum age is 16. Other account-opening requirements may apply.

### Where can I open it?
