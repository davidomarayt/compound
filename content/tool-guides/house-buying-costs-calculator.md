## What this calculator is for

The deposit is only one part of the cash needed to buy a home. This calculator combines the deposit, standard residential Stamp Duty and editable professional/moving costs into one upfront budget.

## How to read the result

Treat the legal, survey, valuation, moving and 'other' fields as your own budget lines rather than official averages. Quotes vary, and the right goal is to avoid being surprised at completion.

## Worked example

On a €400,000 purchase with a 10% deposit, the deposit alone is €40,000. Standard Stamp Duty adds €4,000 before legal fees, valuation, survey, removals or setup costs. The true cash requirement can therefore be meaningfully above €40,000.

## Irish context

Your solicitor handles conveyancing and Stamp Duty filing. Mortgage lenders usually require a valuation, while a structural survey or engineer's inspection is a separate buyer decision. Mortgage protection and buildings insurance are also commonly relevant but are not included automatically because premiums vary.

## What can change the answer

New builds and second-hand homes can have different cost patterns. A new build can require flooring, appliances, snagging or landscaping; an older home can expose repair or retrofit costs. Professional quotes also vary by property complexity.

## Common mistakes to avoid

Do not confuse a lender valuation with a structural survey. Do not spend every euro on completion and leave no reserve. And compare solicitor quotes on an itemised basis, including VAT and outlays, rather than headline fees alone.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are solicitor fees fixed?
No.

### Is a survey mandatory?
A lender valuation is commonly required; a structural survey is a separate matter.

### Does this include mortgage interest?
No.

### Does it include Help to Buy?
No. Scheme support is a funding source rather than a purchase cost.

## Method and limitations

The calculator applies standard residential Stamp Duty to the entered price and adds the deposit plus your editable cost estimates. It does not estimate insurance, renovation, lender-specific charges or scheme eligibility.
