HOW WE BUILD WEALTH CONTENT

Useful numbers. Visible assumptions.

Compound calculators are planning tools, not lender quotes, tax returns, forecasts or personalised investment advice. The assumptions are shown so you can stress-test them rather than treating one output as a promise.

Irish primary sources are prioritised where available, including Revenue, the Central Bank, CCPC, the Pensions Authority and government schemes.
