**Liraglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist.** It is the active ingredient in Saxenda, a daily prescription injection authorised in the EU for weight management in eligible patients alongside changes in diet and physical activity. This guide focuses on adults. [EMA Saxenda overview](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/saxenda) · [HPRA Irish product listing](https://www.hpra.ie/find-a-medicine/for-human-use/authorised-medicines/details/10237)

## The Science Behind the Drug

Liraglutide is a modified version of **glucagon-like peptide-1**, or GLP-1, a hormone involved in appetite and blood glucose regulation. It binds to the **GLP-1 receptor** and activates it. Its structure slows its removal from the body compared with natural GLP-1, allowing it to be used as a once-daily medicine. [EMA Saxenda product information, sections 5.1 and 5.2](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/saxenda-epar-product-information_en.pdf)

GLP-1 receptors are found in parts of the brain involved in appetite. Clinical studies show that liraglutide can increase feelings of fullness and reduce hunger and food intake. Researchers are still working out the full set of pathways behind those effects. [EMA Saxenda product information, section 5.1](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/saxenda-epar-product-information_en.pdf)

## How does Saxenda fit alongside the other medicines?

| Medicine | Receptor target | Administration for the product shown |
|---|---|---|
| Liraglutide (Saxenda) | GLP-1 | Daily injection |
| Semaglutide (Wegovy injection) | GLP-1 | Weekly injection |
| Tirzepatide (Mounjaro) | GIP and GLP-1 | Weekly injection |

Prescribing criteria, formulations and follow-up differ by product. The Saxenda adult prescribing information includes a specific review after 12 weeks at the maintenance dose to assess whether treatment should continue. A prescriber can apply that criterion to your circumstances. [EMA: Saxenda](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/saxenda-epar-product-information_en.pdf) · [EMA: Wegovy](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/wegovy-epar-product-information_en.pdf) · [EMA: Mounjaro](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/mounjaro-epar-product-information_en.pdf)

## What should you watch for?

The Saxenda leaflet lists nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea and constipation among common experiences during treatment. Seek urgent medical advice for severe, persistent abdominal pain, particularly if it spreads to your back. This may be a sign of pancreatitis. Speak to your prescriber about other medicines, pregnancy plans or a procedure that involves general anaesthesia or deep sedation. [EMA Saxenda prescribing information and patient leaflet](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/saxenda-epar-product-information_en.pdf)
