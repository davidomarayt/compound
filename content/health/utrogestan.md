## At a glance

Utrogestan is a brand of micronised progesterone. This guide focuses on its use as part of menopause HRT. Products and routes can differ by indication. [HSE guide](https://www2.hse.ie/medicines/utrogestan/)

## The Science Behind the Drug

### Micronised progesterone and protection of the womb lining

Progesterone binds progesterone receptors and changes gene activity in the endometrium. This opposes the effect of systemic oestrogen on the growth of the womb lining. Micronisation changes the physical particle size to affect absorption. Oral metabolism contributes to effects such as drowsiness. [HSE source](https://www2.hse.ie/medicines/utrogestan/)

## What the evidence measures

Review whether the progesterone schedule matches the oestrogen regimen. Symptoms, sleepiness and bleeding pattern address different questions. A regimen used for fertility care can involve a different product and purpose.

## Read both prescriptions together

| What to check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Oestrogen | exact product and route |
| Progesterone | prescribed route, days used and any symptoms to review |


## Why check both hormones after an HRT change?

A change in oestrogen or progesterone may change how the regimen works as a whole. Ask the prescriber to confirm the route and schedule of both products.

## When to ask for help or a medication review

Check the specific leaflet and discuss unexpected bleeding or difficulty following the schedule. Do not switch route or borrow instructions from another progesterone product. [HSE patient guidance](https://www2.hse.ie/medicines/utrogestan/)
