**Method and caveats:** The CSO combines administrative housing, Stamp Duty, income, property tax, mortgage and Help-to-Buy records; its first-time-buyer classification relies in part on LPT ownership records since 2016, so it can misclassify some purchasers with older or overseas ownership. Non-market transfers, self-builds involving separately purchased land and incomplete matches are excluded by the outlined method. Crucially mortgage records were available for only a subset of transactions, so do not extrapolate mortgage ratios to all first-time buyers. Do **not** independently divide the CSO's 6,020 HTB purchases by 30,480 total transactions and relabel it its reported **18% HTB share** without resolving the release's different effective denominators; quote the official share with its scope. The median-income changes are in **current euros**; no CPI inflation adjustment or causal inference was attempted.
