**Applications opened on 23 November for 3,850 Census 2027 Field Support Officer jobs across Ireland. The temporary role runs for eight weeks, averages 18 hours a week and pays more than €3,000 plus an allowance. The competition can close before its stated deadline once 12,000 applications are received.**

These are the jobs previously called census enumerator roles. They are mainly local, part-time positions involving visits to households, use of a census field app and follow-up work after the census completion period.

The official closing time is **2pm on Friday 4 December 2026**, but that is not a guaranteed application window. The Census 2027 site says the campaign will close when it reaches 12,000 applications, even if that happens earlier.

## Pay, hours and dates

| Term | Official campaign detail |
| --- | --- |
| Number of jobs | 3,850 nationwide |
| Contract | Eight weeks, 5 April to 28 May 2027 |
| Normal weekly hours | 18, mainly evenings and weekends |
| Advertised pay | More than €3,000 for the full contract, plus an allowance |
| Minimum age | 18 |
| Application deadline | 2pm, 4 December 2026, or earlier at 12,000 applications |

Eighteen hours over eight weeks is **144 scheduled hours**:

> 18 hours × 8 weeks = **144 hours**

Using exactly €3,000 as a conservative floor produces an implied gross rate of just over **€20.83 per scheduled hour**:

> €3,000 ÷ 144 = **€20.83**

The campaign says the actual total is more than €3,000 and includes an allowance, so €20.83 is not the quoted contractual hourly rate. It is only a way to compare the advertised minimum total with the stated weekly hours. Tax, travel, the allowance terms and the final contract can change what the job is worth to an individual applicant.

## What the work involves

Field Support Officers will work in or near their local area. Between **5 April and 9 May**, they will call to homes to explain Census 2027 and offer help. After 9 May, they will follow up with households that have not completed a form.

The CSO says each officer should expect responsibility for around **450 to 850 homes**. Some addresses may require repeat visits. A mobile phone with the census field app will be supplied, and online completions will automatically come off the officer's visit list.

This is not simply a form-delivery job. The 2027 census will be the first Irish census in which households can choose to respond online, and officers may also need to engage with local groups that need help understanding or completing the census.

## How competitive could it be?

If the campaign receives the full 12,000 applications for 3,850 positions, that is approximately **3.1 applications per job**:

> 12,000 ÷ 3,850 = **3.12**

That ratio is not a forecast of an individual applicant's chance. Jobs are distributed across local areas, applications are shortlisted and shortlisted candidates are interviewed. Demand can therefore vary significantly by location.

## Before applying

1. Check that evening and weekend work fits around your existing job or caring responsibilities.
2. Consider whether repeated household visits and responsibility for up to 850 addresses are practical for you.
3. Read the official campaign terms before relying on the €3,000 headline, particularly the allowance, expenses and tax treatment.
4. Apply through the official [Census 2027 recruitment link](https://recruitment.census.ie/) rather than a copied advertisement.
5. Submit early if interested because the 12,000-application cap can close the campaign before 4 December.

For comparing a separate job offer with your usual pay, Compound's [salary-to-hourly calculator](/salary-hourly-rate-calculator/) converts annual, monthly or weekly income into an hourly figure. For this census role, the final campaign booklet and contract should take priority over any simple division.

The useful headline is not only that 3,850 jobs are available. It is that this is a short, locally based contract with an unusual work pattern, a defined household workload and an application cap that makes the advertised closing date conditional.
