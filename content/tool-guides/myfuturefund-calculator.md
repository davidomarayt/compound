## What this calculator is for

MyFutureFund is Ireland's automatic-enrolment retirement savings system. This calculator estimates contributions from the employee, employer and State under the phased contribution schedule.

## How to read the result

The useful number is the **total amount entering the account**, not only the deduction from take-home pay. In 2026–2028, the employee contributes 1.5% of eligible gross pay, the employer contributes 1.5% and the State adds 0.5%.

## Worked example

On €45,000 of eligible annual pay in 2026, the employee contribution is €675, the employer adds €675 and the State adds €225, giving €1,575 total for the year. Official government examples use the same structure.

## Irish context

The published phase-in is 1.5%/1.5%/0.5% in 2026–28, 3%/3%/1% in 2029–31, 4.5%/4.5%/1.5% in 2032–34 and 6%/6%/2% from 2035 for employee/employer/State respectively. Eligibility depends on age, earnings and whether qualifying pension contributions already exist for the employment. Contributions are subject to the scheme's earnings threshold rules.

## What can change the answer

Salary, multiple employments, existing pension arrangements and the phase of the scheme all affect contributions. The State top-up is not the same as ordinary pension Income Tax relief, so comparisons with PRSAs or occupational pensions need care.

## Common mistakes to avoid

Do not compare only the employee deduction with a private pension contribution while ignoring employer matching and the State top-up. Do not assume all salary above the scheme earnings threshold attracts contributions. And do not assume auto-enrolment replaces the need to think about retirement adequacy.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the 2026 rates?
Employee 1.5%, employer 1.5%, State 0.5% on eligible pay.

### Do rates stay at 1.5% forever?
No. They phase upward over the first decade.

### Is the State top-up ordinary tax relief?
No. It is a separate scheme contribution.

### What if I already contribute to a pension through payroll?
That can affect whether the employment is enrolled; check the current MyFutureFund eligibility rules.

## Method and limitations

The calculator applies the statutory contribution rates for the relevant phase to eligible earnings under the assumptions implemented in the tool. It does not determine legal enrolment status for every employment or predict investment performance.
