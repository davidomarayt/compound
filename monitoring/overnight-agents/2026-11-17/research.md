**Method and caveats:** Source figures from CSO Tables 1, 3a and 4a in the release; figures rounded to nearest 100, so arithmetic differences are approximate. Published index growth is calculated on unrounded data. CSO explicitly says a **significant proportion** of NACE O/Q monthly and annual movements reflects certain HSE employees being re-registered under an employer number assigned a different NACE sector. It does *not* quantify the exact reclassified subset; no inference that +29,800 represents civil-service jobs created or -10,400 represents healthcare jobs lost is justified. Recent two months are provisional and the series/methodology may be revised. The LFS remains the official employment measure; payroll employee counts exclude other forms of work and should not be substituted for total jobs or unemployment.
