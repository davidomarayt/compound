## How to check medicine information in Ireland

The [Health Products Regulatory Authority (HPRA)](https://www.hpra.ie/find-a-medicine) provides Irish medicine authorisation details, patient information leaflets and product characteristics. Instructions can differ by product, strength, age and medical circumstances. Always check the leaflet for your exact product.

These guides provide general education, not individual medical advice. Ask a pharmacist or clinician about your circumstances. If you suspect an overdose, seek urgent medical advice even if you feel well.
