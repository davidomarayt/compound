## What this calculator is for

Local Property Tax is an annual self-assessed tax on residential property. This calculator estimates the charge from the property's value and the Local Adjustment Factor used by the selected local authority.

## How to read the result

The result is driven by the property's valuation band, or by percentage rates for higher-value properties, and then adjusted locally. Your mortgage balance and personal income do not reduce the property value used for LPT.

## Worked example

Two homes with the same market value can have different final LPT bills if their local authorities apply different adjustments. That is why the authority field matters as much as the valuation band.

## Irish context

Revenue administers LPT nationally, while local authorities can vary the basic rate within the permitted framework. Liability, exemptions, deferrals and valuation rules are separate from the arithmetic shown by this tool.

## What can change the answer

A change in property value can move a home into a different band. Local-authority decisions can also change the final charge. High-value properties use a different percentage structure from ordinary valuation bands.

## Common mistakes to avoid

Do not use the original purchase price automatically if it no longer reflects the relevant valuation basis. Do not subtract the mortgage balance from the property value. And do not assume the calculator establishes whether you qualify for an exemption or deferral.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a mortgage reduce LPT?
No.

### Is LPT identical across Ireland?
No. Local Adjustment Factors can change the final amount.

### Does this calculator value my home?
No. You supply the value.

### Does it determine exemptions?
No. Check Revenue for liability and exemption rules.

## Method and limitations

The tool maps the entered property value to the implemented LPT band or high-value formula, then applies the local-authority adjustment. It does not determine legal liability, exemptions, deferrals or the correct valuation evidence.
