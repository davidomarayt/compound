03 / WHY TWO NUMBERS CAN BOTH BE CORRECT

Net supply grants are not gross sector spending.

The {{ spending.vote_count }} Votes are legal spending authorisations in a defined parliamentary resolution. The broad sector figures elsewhere on Compound also include funding recorded through other channels.

Gross sector figures

The official 2026 public spending explorer shows approximately €133bn of gross voted and non-voted expenditure, grouped into eight user-facing sectors.

Net voted grants

Parliament votes net Exchequer supply requirements, with appropriations-in-aid and Social Insurance Fund arrangements affecting the relationship to gross programme totals.

Example: Social Protection

Vote 37: €14.066bn net grant. The broader social-protection category is €28.9bn gross. They have different accounting definitions, so the difference must not be casually labelled as an exact PRSI total.

Programme records

Our programme allocation explorer covers 49 specific funding statements and projections. Some are nested inside votes or each other; we never add overlapping allocations to the net grants.

Data status and exclusions

The original Dáil resolution is dated 25 March 2026 and records limits on Exchequer supply grants. It is not a cash-spending return or a complete list of all current and capital subheads. Separate vote approvals, extra capital carryover authorisations and in-year revised/supplementary estimates may exist. The register is a fixed snapshot of that resolution rather than a continuously reconciled 2026 total.

Verified {{ spending.vote_date }}. The supporting financial definitions are explained by the Parliamentary Budget Office's Revised Estimates analysis.
