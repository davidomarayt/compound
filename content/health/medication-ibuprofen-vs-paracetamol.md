**Both ibuprofen and paracetamol can relieve pain and fever, but they work differently and have different safety considerations.** Ibuprofen is an NSAID with anti-inflammatory effects; paracetamol is primarily used for pain and fever.

| Question | Ibuprofen | Paracetamol |
|---|---|---|
| Helps relieve pain? | Yes | Yes |
| Reduces fever? | Yes | Yes |
| Reduces inflammation? | Yes | Not generally used for this |
| Important risks | Stomach bleeding, kidney and cardiovascular effects, certain asthma reactions | Liver injury from overdose or duplicate ingredients |
| Typical product examples | Some Nurofen products | Some Panadol products |

## Which is better?

There is no universal winner. The type of pain, other medicines, medical history, pregnancy status and product instructions all affect which may be appropriate. A pharmacist can help choose safely.

## Can you take them together?

Some authorised Irish medicines contain both ingredients, but **that does not mean every combination or dose is appropriate**. Check with a pharmacist and follow the leaflet for the specific product. Avoid taking multiple products containing the same ingredient.
