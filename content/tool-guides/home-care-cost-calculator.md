## Start with the visits that need to be covered

Write down what help is needed in an ordinary week and when it is needed. List mornings, evenings, weekends and any overnight cover separately before calling providers. Use the calculator to put a price on that schedule. Ask your care team what kind of support and staffing the person needs. The budget can compare two arrangements, but that assessment is what tells you whether either arrangement is suitable.

A rate alone does not tell you the cost of a visit. Ask how much the provider would invoice for the exact schedule. For example, a 30-minute visit may be billed as a longer minimum visit. In that case enter the billed hours and an equivalent rate, taking care that the hours you deduct for HSE support correspond to entire privately billed visits they replace. If a one-hour minimum remains payable after part of the visit is covered, use the actual remaining private charge. Ask for an itemised written quote for weekday mornings, evening or weekend calls, bank holidays, and any regular travel or service charge.

## How HSE Home Support affects what you pay

The HSE says its Home Support Service for older people is free and has no income test or medical card requirement. The care offered depends on the person’s needs. You can apply while the person is at home or in hospital, or if they are in a nursing home and want to return home. Some people under 65 may also qualify. The HSE can deliver approved support directly or through an approved provider. [Read the HSE’s Home Support Service guidance](https://www2.hse.ie/services/home-support-service/home-support-service-for-older-people/).

Ask what has been approved, which visits can actually be provided and when they can start. Enter only confirmed hours that replace care on the same part of your schedule. Five confirmed weekday morning hours cannot cover a Sunday visit. While support is pending, use zero confirmed hours to see the full private cost. If you have a confirmed start date, Advanced mode can include the weeks you pay privately before that date.

Under Consumer Directed Home Support, the HSE may confirm a weekly funding amount. Ask the approved provider what hours that amount buys at the times you need. Enter those confirmed hours here. If you arrange additional private care, ask for a separate quote that makes the private charges clear.

## What private home care costs can include

Two providers’ advertised rates illustrate why it is worth checking the full quote. Viola Care advertises €21.50 per hour for its Dublin service and says it quotes individually for long-term or high-hours care. Hibernia lists a basic private hourly rate of €34.90 in its FAQ. These are examples from individual providers checked in November 2026. Availability, care arrangements and what is included can differ. The calculator’s €30 starting rate is an illustration you can replace. [Viola’s Dublin rates](https://www.violacare.ie/dublin) · [Hibernia’s FAQ](https://hiberniahomecare.ie/faqs/).

For overnight care, ask whether the quote covers a sleeping shift or an awake carer. If you have a flat cost for a whole night, divide the charge by the quoted billed hours and enter those hours under overnight care. If two carers must attend together, ask for the combined price and enter a rate that reflects both carers for those clock hours. For a live-in or round-the-clock arrangement, get the full breakdown of cover, breaks and relief staffing before using an hourly equivalent. Different schedules can meet very different needs even when their hours cost the same.

## Worked example with HSE hours and a start date

Suppose the regular schedule is 14 hours at €30 per hour. Paying privately for all of it costs **€420 per week**. If five confirmed HSE hours replace five of those hours, nine private hours remain at **€270 per week**, which averages **€1,170 per calendar month** over 52 weeks. Six weeks of full private payment before the confirmed support starts would put the first-year budget at **€14,940**, assuming the same schedule and rate throughout.

If a weekend visit or overnight shift is quoted separately, use Advanced mode. Enter the hours only once, with confirmed HSE hours assigned to the same time band. Enter recurring extras separately only if they apply before and after support begins. Save your first scenario for comparison before you price another timetable.

## What to ask before agreeing to a care plan

Ask who will cover the visits if a regular carer is sick or on holiday, whether short-notice cancellations are charged, and how soon the provider can increase or reduce visits. Ask how visits are documented and who the family should contact with a concern. Confirm what happens on bank holidays, whether a different rate applies at night, and what notice is needed to change or end an arrangement. A written answer helps a family compare cost and reliability on the same basis.

If you are paying privately for care for someone who meets Revenue’s incapacity conditions, check whether relief for employing a carer may apply. The calculator shows the cash cost before any relief. Confirm who pays, who keeps invoices, and whether you have any obligations when hiring someone directly. [Revenue’s employing-a-carer guide](https://www.revenue.ie/en/personal-tax-credits-reliefs-and-exemptions/health-and-age/employing-a-carer/index.aspx).

## Compare home care with residential care

Use the same time period for both budgets. For care at home, list other costs that still apply, such as meals, household bills, equipment and backup support, as well as paid visits. Take account of time family members have agreed to cover and discuss what happens if they can no longer do so. For residential care, use the written fee, the amount you would pay after any confirmed Fair Deal support and any extras.

Compound’s [Nursing Home Cost Calculator](/nursing-home-cost-calculator/) shows the household budget for residential care. [FairPath’s nursing home finder](https://fairpath.ie/nursing-homes-near-me) helps you look up homes by county and explore prices and inspection report previews. FairPath is a sister site to Compound and sells full inspection summaries where available. A financial comparison can help prepare a discussion with the person receiving care, their family and their care team.

## Method and limitations

The calculator multiplies needed weekly hours by the private rate for each schedule. It subtracts confirmed HSE hours from the same schedule up to the number needed, then prices the remaining hours. Identical entered extras are added in each scenario. The first year includes 52 weeks, using the full private price for the weeks entered before support begins. The average calendar month is the ongoing weekly amount multiplied by 52 and divided by 12. It uses only the prices and hours entered. Rate changes, tax relief, and costs outside the schedule are separate from this estimate.

## Frequently asked questions

### What if support has been approved but I do not know the timetable?

Use the full private quote for your immediate budget. Once you know which visits are covered, enter those hours and the expected start date.

### Can I include weekend and overnight care?

Yes. Use Advanced mode and enter any separately priced hours and corresponding HSE coverage in their own fields. Follow the quote when the same visit spans two rates.

### Does the budget include Fair Deal?

This tool is for home support in the Republic of Ireland. To budget for residential care, use the separate [Nursing Home Cost Calculator](/nursing-home-cost-calculator/).
