  <section aria-labelledby="ins-compare-heading">
    <div class="ins-section-head">
      <div><p class="ins-ey">START WITH THE PURPOSE</p><h2 id="ins-compare-heading">They do not all cover the same thing.</h2></div>
      <p>Understanding what triggers a claim matters more than selecting the lowest advertised monthly premium.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="cs-table-wrap" role="region" tabindex="0" aria-label="Comparison of Irish insurance protection types">
      <table>
        <thead><tr><th scope="col">Protection</th><th scope="col">Main purpose</th><th scope="col">Usually does not cover</th></tr></thead>
        <tbody>
          <tr><th scope="row">Life insurance</th><td>Eligible death during the insured term, or throughout life for qualifying whole-of-life contracts.</td><td>Ordinary life-only cover does not pay automatically on illness diagnosis or job loss.</td></tr>
          <tr><th scope="row">Mortgage protection</th><td>Help repay the mortgage following an insured borrower's death.</td><td>Ordinary cover does not replace monthly wages or routine household spending.</td></tr>
          <tr><th scope="row">Income protection</th><td>Replace part of eligible earnings if sickness or injury prevents working after the deferred period.</td><td>Unemployment, general redundancy or all pre-illness earnings.</td></tr>
          <tr><th scope="row">Private health insurance</th><td>Contribute towards specific covered healthcare costs and access arrangements.</td><td>A general income-replacement lump sum or universal payment for every treatment.</td></tr>
        </tbody>
      </table>
    </div>
    <p class="ins-disclaimer">Every insurer and policy defines benefits, eligibility, exclusions and limits differently. Review policy wordings and the current independent guidance of the <a href="https://www.ccpc.ie/">CCPC</a>. For Irish private health insurance plans, use the <a href="https://www.hia.ie/health-insurance-comparison">Health Insurance Authority comparison tool</a>.</p>

  <section aria-labelledby="ins-faq-heading">
    <div class="ins-section-head"><div><p class="ins-ey">INSURANCE FAQ</p><h2 id="ins-faq-heading">Before you compare quotes.</h2></div></div>
    <div class="ins-faq">
      <details><summary>Do I have to buy life insurance in Ireland?</summary><p>General personal life insurance is optional. Mortgage protection is normally required for a mortgage on your main home, subject to statutory exceptions. Learn about the distinctions in the <a href="/wealth/mortgage-protection-insurance-ireland/">mortgage protection guide</a>.</p></details>
      <details><summary>Do these calculators give insurance quotes?</summary><p>No. These tools estimate needs and compare arithmetic scenarios only. Insurers price policies using factors such as cover amount, term, age, health, tobacco use and underwriting decisions.</p></details>
      <details><summary>Can I choose an insurer other than my bank's?</summary><p>For suitable mortgage protection, borrowers can normally choose a provider other than their mortgage lender, provided the lender accepts the cover and assignment arrangements. Compare terms and final accepted premiums before switching.</p></details>
      <details><summary>Can I rely on death-in-service cover alone?</summary><p>It may reduce your additional cover needs, but verify its amount, eligibility, nominated beneficiaries and what happens if you change job or retire. Only count benefits genuinely available to survivors.</p></details>
