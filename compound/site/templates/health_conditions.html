How to read the Irish figures

Prevalence estimates how many people have a condition in a defined population and time frame. New diagnoses and notifications count events during a reporting period. Hospital and registry figures describe the people or encounters captured by those systems.

Each figure gives the population, period and original source. A missing figure means Compound has yet to verify one for this entry. The HSE index also includes symptoms, tests, treatments and services, which may have a different type of data.
