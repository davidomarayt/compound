02 / READ THE RIGHT NUMBER

Why the figure may differ from the sector total.

The spending atlas is based on gross public expenditure. The amount above is a net voted Exchequer supply grant. They describe different things and cannot be interchanged.

This Vote: {{ vote.amount_display }}

The 2026 Dáil-approved net supply-grant ceiling for this specified department or agency, under the 25 March resolution.

Sector: €{{ "%.1f"|format(vote.sector_record.billion) }}bn

The rounded {{ vote.sector_record.name|lower }} sector gross expenditure estimate in the separate official sector dataset. It is not an amount paid from this Vote alone.

Current and capital

Operational costs and longer-lived investment can both be included in voted expenditure. This resolution provides the net grant, not an itemised, reconciled current/capital subhead table for this individual Vote.

Social insurance and non-voted items

Government expenditure can include spending from the Social Insurance Fund, the National Training Fund and direct Central Fund charges. Their treatment differs from ordinary annual Vote supply grants.
