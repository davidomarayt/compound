An estimate, not an outturn.

The 2026 voted figures use Revised Estimates; non-voted amounts use fiscal projections. Do not describe them as final money spent. The source snapshot was updated in October 2026.

A budget is not an outcome.

This dataset does not prove what was delivered, assess value for money or measure service quality. It is the funding side of that investigation.

Shares are illustrative.

Applying the national spending mix to your payroll contribution is a comparison, not tracing. It is not a statement that PRSI is earmarked across these programmes.
