*Updated 23 November 2026 following Nvidia's confirmation that Huang had returned.*

**Jensen Huang is back as Nvidia's chief executive.** Nvidia's board voted unanimously to reinstate its co-founder as president, CEO and a director, effective immediately on Monday, 23 November 2026. Colette Kress, who ran the company on an interim basis, continues as chief financial officer. Harvey C. Jones remains board chair. [Nvidia's board announcement](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Board-Reinstates-Jensen-Huang-as-President-CEO-and-Board-Director/default.aspx)

**Nvidia shares rose about 33% in early U.S. trading**, Reuters reported. The rally shows how heavily the leadership question had weighed on the stock. For shareholders, the next test is whether Nvidia can keep delivering on chip sales and margins while its independent inquiry and government scrutiny continue. [Reuters market report via Euronext](https://live.euronext.com/en/financial-news/nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-ceo-igniting-tech-surge)

## Why did Nvidia bring Jensen Huang back?

Nvidia announced Huang's resignation on 12 November and appointed Kress interim CEO. On 17 November, Huang said he was willing to return if the board asked. Reuters reported that the board had publicly opposed his return days before Monday's reversal and that U.S. President Donald Trump had urged Nvidia to reinstate him. [Compound's coverage of the resignation](/news/jensen-huang-resigns-nvidia-ceo/) · [Reuters on the board reversal](https://www.reuters.com/technology/nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-ceo-2026-11-23/)

Nvidia said directors met with Huang and received input from senior U.S. government officials before voting. The board said it was satisfied with his candour and ability to lead and concluded that his reinstatement posed no national-security risk. The independent committee's investigation into reports that Huang was targeted by a foreign intelligence effort will continue, and Nvidia said it would keep cooperating with government investigations and inquiries. [Nvidia's announcement filed with the SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000090/nov23pressrelease.htm)

## Why the stock rallied

Huang has been closely involved in Nvidia's chip roadmap, major customer relationships and dealings with Washington. Bringing him back gives customers and suppliers a clearer picture of who will make decisions as Nvidia rolls out its Vera Rubin platform. The size of the rally also gives the board a reason to pay attention to succession planning. If investors attach that much value to one executive's presence, any future leadership transition can move the stock well before it moves earnings. [Reuters on Huang's role](https://www.reuters.com/technology/nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-ceo-2026-11-23/)

The earnings figures show how much still depends on execution. Nvidia reported **$115.8 billion** of revenue for the quarter ended 25 October, including **$107.6 billion** from Data Center, about **93%** of the total. Its fiscal fourth-quarter revenue outlook is **$130 billion**, plus or minus 3%, for a quarter with an extra week. The forecast assumes zero Data Center compute revenue from China. [Nvidia's earnings release](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Third-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/default.aspx) · [Compound's NVDA earnings and stock profile](/stocks/nvda/)

## What investors should watch next

- **Vera Rubin sales and gross margin.** Nvidia says it has begun shipping its Vera Rubin NVL72 system. It expects fourth-quarter GAAP gross margin of **71% to 72%**, compared with **74.8%** in the third quarter. Delivering the revenue outlook and explaining how margins develop during the rollout will matter for the next earnings report. [Nvidia's results and outlook](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Third-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/default.aspx)
- **China sales and export licences.** Huang's return puts a familiar executive back in discussions with Washington. Export licensing and actual shipments will determine whether Nvidia can book sales in China beyond what it included in its forecast. [Compound's analysis of Nvidia's export-control exposure](/news/nvidia-national-security-export-control-committee-2026/)
- **The inquiry and compliance changes.** Nvidia said its independent investigation would proceed and that it was searching for a chief compliance officer who would report to the board's nominating and corporate governance committee. The findings and any changes the board makes will help investors assess how the company handles future governance risks. [Nvidia's SEC-filed announcement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000090/nov23pressrelease.htm)
