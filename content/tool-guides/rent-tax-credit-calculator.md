## What this calculator is for

The Rent Tax Credit reduces Income Tax for qualifying renters. This calculator estimates the credit from the rent and taxpayer circumstances entered, subject to the annual cap and available Income Tax liability.

## How to read the result

A tax credit cannot generally create an Income Tax refund beyond tax that would otherwise be due. The amount of rent paid is therefore only one constraint; eligibility and Income Tax liability also matter.

## Worked example

If a qualifying renter's calculated credit is €1,000 but they have only €600 of Income Tax liability available to offset, the usable credit can be limited by that tax liability.

## Irish context

Revenue states that the Rent Tax Credit is available for tax years 2022 to 2028 and can be claimed in-year for 2026 by PAYE taxpayers through myAccount. Conditions differ depending on whether the rented property is your principal private residence, a second home used for work/education or accommodation rented for a child.

## What can change the answer

Rent paid, number of eligible claimants, Income Tax liability, tenancy circumstances and relationship between tenant/landlord can all matter. Rules have changed since the credit was introduced, so use current Revenue guidance.

## Common mistakes to avoid

Do not assume every euro of rent produces a euro of credit. Do not confuse the credit with a rent subsidy paid by the landlord. And do not assume eligibility if the tenancy does not meet Revenue's conditions.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I claim it during 2026?
PAYE taxpayers can claim in-year through Revenue's current process.

### Does it reduce USC or PRSI?
It is an Income Tax credit.

### Is it available through 2028?
Revenue currently states the credit applies for tax years 2022–2028.

### Does the calculator prove eligibility?
No.

## Method and limitations

The calculator applies the implemented annual credit limit to the qualifying rent/tax assumptions entered. It does not validate tenancy registration, landlord relationships, duplicate claims or every eligibility condition.
