Pick a completed tax year in Revenue’s myAccount. Compare the credits and income Revenue shows with your own records, then add eligible claims one at a time. This checklist covers common items for employees and pensioners in the PAYE system.

## Rent Tax Credit {#rent-tax-credit}

Check the tenancy and payment conditions and gather proof of rent paid. Have your tenancy details and the Residential Tenancies Board registration number if relevant to your tenancy. Use the [Rent Tax Credit guide](/wealth/rent-tax-credit-ireland-who-can-claim/) and <a data-claim-tool="rent-tax-credit" href="/rent-tax-credit-calculator/">estimate the credit</a> before following [Revenue’s rules for that year](https://www.revenue.ie/en/personal-tax-credits-reliefs-and-exemptions/land-and-property/rent-credit/index.aspx).

## Health expenses {#health-expenses}

Gather receipts for qualifying medical expenses and subtract reimbursements, including those from health insurers. Check which dental treatments qualify and get the relevant documentation where required. For example, if €300 in qualifying expenses was partly reimbursed by €100, the eligible balance is €200. At 20%, that would give **€40 in relief** if the claim qualifies and you paid sufficient tax. [Check Revenue’s health expenses guidance](https://www.revenue.ie/en/personal-tax-credits-reliefs-and-exemptions/health-and-age/health-expenses/index.aspx).

## Work expenses and pension contributions {#work-expenses}

- **Working from home.** Gather utility bills, count eligible home-working days and note relevant employer reimbursements before checking [Remote Working Relief](https://www.revenue.ie/en/jobs-and-pensions/eworking/index.aspx).
- **Flat-rate expenses.** Look up your occupation and duties on [Revenue’s flat-rate expense list](https://www.revenue.ie/en/personal-tax-credits-reliefs-and-exemptions/income-and-employment/flat-rate-expenses/index.aspx). Check your tax record before claiming for a year in which the allowance might already have been included.
- **Personal pension contributions.** Check your contribution statement and payroll records. If you paid qualifying contributions directly, check [Revenue’s pension relief rules and limits](https://www.revenue.ie/en/jobs-and-pensions/pension/index.aspx).

## Tuition fees {#tuition-fees}

Check the institution, course and type of fee against [Revenue’s tuition fee rules](https://www.revenue.ie/en/personal-tax-credits-reliefs-and-exemptions/education/tuition-fees-paid-for-third-level-education/index.aspx). Keep the fee statement and payment records. Check how grants, employer payments and the relevant disregard affect the qualifying amount.

## File through Revenue myAccount

1. Sign in to [Revenue myAccount](https://www.ros.ie/myaccount-web/sign_in.html) and choose **PAYE Services**.
2. Select **Review your tax for the previous 4 years**, choose the relevant year and request a review. File a separate return for each year.
3. Verify employment, pension and other income, add the eligible credits or reliefs you identified, and submit the return.
4. Open the **Statement of Liability** when it arrives. It confirms whether you overpaid, underpaid or paid the correct amount. [Follow Revenue’s PAYE return instructions](https://www.revenue.ie/en/jobs-and-pensions/end-of-year-process/paye-income-tax-return.aspx).

**Check your oldest claim year first.** Revenue generally applies a [four-year time limit](https://www.revenue.ie/en/personal-tax-credits-reliefs-and-exemptions/four-year-rule/index.aspx). For example, a claim relating to the 2022 tax year generally needs to reach Revenue by **31 December 2026**. Keep your receipts and supporting records for each year you claim.
