## What this calculator is for

Rent versus buy is not a comparison between rent and the mortgage payment alone. Buying combines financing, ownership costs and asset accumulation; renting buys housing flexibility without creating property equity.

## How to read the result

Use the result as a **scenario comparison**, not a prediction. The answer depends heavily on time horizon, house-price growth, rent growth, mortgage rate, maintenance, transaction costs and the return assumed on money that remains invested instead of being used as a deposit.

## Worked example

A buyer may pay €1,800 a month on a mortgage while a renter pays €1,600. The €200 difference does not settle the question: part of the mortgage payment reduces principal, while the buyer also faces stamp duty, maintenance and selling costs. Over ten years, those moving parts matter more than the first month's cash-flow gap.

## Irish context

Irish buyers face Stamp Duty, legal fees, LPT and mortgage rules; renters may qualify for the Rent Tax Credit if conditions are met. Property prices and rents can vary substantially by location, so national averages are a poor substitute for your own scenario.

## What can change the answer

Time horizon is critical. Short ownership periods make transaction costs more important. High house-price growth favours ownership in the model; high investment returns can favour renting if the renter genuinely invests the difference. Higher mortgage rates can shift the result quickly.

## Common mistakes to avoid

Do not count the full mortgage payment as a pure cost because principal builds equity. Do not assume property always rises. Do not assume investment returns are guaranteed. And do not ignore the possibility that your housing needs change.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does buying always win over a long period?
No.

### Is mortgage principal a cost?
It is a cash outflow but also reduces debt and builds equity.

### Should I assume house-price growth?
Run several scenarios, including conservative ones.

### What if I may move in a few years?
Short horizons make buying costs particularly important.

## Method and limitations

The calculator compares the entered renting and ownership assumptions over time. It cannot predict future property prices, rents, investment returns, interest rates or tax rules.
