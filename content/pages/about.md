Compound is an Irish site about three things that compound: your health, your wealth and your happiness.

The spine of the site is Irish grants, tax and money news, written for the person paying rather than the professional advising. Every figure in every piece is traced back to its source, and nothing goes live until it has been read and approved by a human.

It is written by David O'Mara between rotating 12-hour shifts.
