**Nvidia co-founder Jensen Huang has resigned as chief executive officer, president and board director, effective immediately on Thursday, 12 November 2026.** Nvidia confirmed the change in an [official press release](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition). Long-serving chief financial officer **Colette Kress** has become interim CEO, while the board searches for a permanent successor.

The resignation is a major governance event for the company behind much of the global AI-computing boom. Yet shareholders need to separate the immediate leadership shock from the underlying economics of Nvidia's chip business. The first is confirmed; the impact on orders, margins and cash flow remains to be demonstrated.

## Nvidia CEO resignation: what happened on 12 November?

Nvidia's board made three decisions public in its 12 November announcement:

- **Jensen Huang resigned** as president, CEO and director with immediate effect.
- **Colette Kress was appointed interim CEO.** She remains executive vice president and CFO during the transition.
- **Harvey C. Jones became Nvidia's first board chair.** The company said its permanent CEO search will consider both internal and external candidates.

Huang co-founded Nvidia in 1993 and led it through the rise of programmable graphics, accelerated computing and AI infrastructure. Replacing an executive who has combined technical strategy, customer relationships and public leadership for more than three decades creates an unusual succession challenge.

Nvidia said that its board expects operations, customer commitments and the technology roadmap to continue without interruption. This is **management's stated expectation**, not yet evidence that a transition will be financially seamless. [Source: Nvidia's 12 November announcement](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition).

## Why did Jensen Huang resign from Nvidia?

The company **confirmed Huang's resignation but did not specify a definitive cause** in its 12 November transition release. The timing follows days of news reporting about Huang's personal conduct and potential national-security concerns.

On 8 November, Nvidia's board [announced an independent investigation](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/statement-from-nvidia-board-of-directors) into reports that Huang had been targeted by a foreign intelligence effort. It said at the time that it had no reason to believe either national security or Nvidia secrets had been compromised. On 10 November, the independent directors [identified outside investigators](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/statement-from-the-independent-members-of-the-nvidia-board) who would oversee the inquiry.

Those statements confirm the inquiry, **not the underlying allegations**. It would be inaccurate to describe allegations about espionage or wrongdoing as proven, or to claim that the board has published final investigative findings. Compound's [earlier report on the Huang governance concerns](/news/nvidia-huang-governance-investor-risk/) explains what had been reported before the resignation became official.

For shareholders, the unresolved questions are whether the investigation identifies material operational or compliance issues, whether customers see disruptions and how convincingly the board explains its succession arrangements.

## Who is Colette Kress, Nvidia's new interim CEO?

Kress has been Nvidia's chief financial officer since September 2013. She previously held senior finance responsibilities at Cisco and Microsoft, and earlier worked at Texas Instruments.

That experience matters because she already knows Nvidia's balance sheet, financial reporting, capital allocation and investor communications. She is not, however, being presented by the company as the permanent replacement for Huang. **She currently holds both the interim CEO and CFO positions**, and the board is still searching.

The next permanent chief executive will inherit more than a semiconductor product portfolio. Nvidia's competitive position also depends on the CUDA software ecosystem, networking, supply commitments, partnerships with the world's largest technology buyers and navigating export restrictions.

## What does Jensen Huang's resignation mean for NVDA stock?

The market may reassess Nvidia along **two distinct dimensions**:

1. **Execution risk:** a leadership transition could eventually affect product decisions, engineering retention, customer confidence or regulatory relationships. Investors should look for evidence in disclosures and results, rather than assume deterioration has already occurred.
2. **Valuation risk:** even if near-term profits remain unchanged, investors could demand a lower earnings multiple to compensate for uncertainty around governance and succession.

For example, a hypothetical company earning $10 per share would be valued at $300 per share at 30 times earnings, but $270 at 27 times earnings. That is a **10% valuation change without any change in the earnings assumption**. These figures are illustrative, **not an Nvidia share-price forecast or analyst target**.

Early reports on 12 November described a sharp fall in Nvidia shares in pre-market US trading. Pre-market quotes can move rapidly and should not be confused with a confirmed market close or a lasting change in fundamental value. The [live NVDA company page](/stocks/nvda/) is a more appropriate place to check current market information.

An abrupt CEO departure creates a credible case for a higher governance-risk discount. Whether that discount persists will depend on management continuity, the independent review, buyer behaviour and earnings.

## Nvidia's latest financial numbers: what is at stake?

The business entering this leadership transition is not the Nvidia of a decade ago. In the **second quarter of fiscal 2027**, reported on 26 August 2026, Nvidia disclosed:

| Nvidia fiscal Q2 2027 metric | Reported result |
|---|---:|
| Total quarterly revenue | **$96.2 billion** |
| Year-on-year revenue growth | **106%** |
| Data Center revenue | **$89.0 billion** |
| Data Center year-on-year growth | **117%** |
| GAAP gross margin | **75.0%** |
| GAAP diluted earnings per share | **$2.46** |

Source: [Nvidia's official fiscal Q2 2027 earnings release](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-second-quarter-fiscal-2027). Figures are rounded as shown by the company.

Data Center represented approximately **92.5% of reported quarterly revenue** ($89.0 billion divided by $96.2 billion). That concentration makes AI-infrastructure purchasing decisions especially important to Nvidia's future earnings.

Nvidia's previous outlook for fiscal Q3 called for **$108 billion in revenue, plus or minus 2%**, and a **74.0% GAAP and non-GAAP gross margin, plus or minus 0.5 percentage points**. Those were management's forecasts issued in August, **not results that have been reported**. They should not be treated as an updated prediction from the new interim CEO.

At the $108 billion revenue guidance midpoint, a one-percentage-point gross-margin difference would equal roughly **$1.08 billion of quarterly gross profit**, before operating expenses and tax. This arithmetic illustrates why product mix and pricing can matter more to Nvidia's long-term earnings than a single day's share-price reaction.

## Nvidia earnings on 17 November are the next major test

Nvidia has confirmed that its **third-quarter fiscal 2027 results remain scheduled for Tuesday, 17 November 2026**, at approximately 1:20 p.m. Pacific Time, followed by an earnings call at 2 p.m. Pacific Time. [Official timetable](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition).

Investors should pay particular attention to four things: whether Q3 revenue meets the previous outlook; whether gross margins and product deliveries remain on track; how Kress and the board describe operating responsibilities during the transition; and whether management changes its commentary on demand, capital allocation or the technology roadmap.

A robust quarter would help establish that the underlying business can maintain momentum through the transition. It would not, on its own, resolve governance concerns. Conversely, weaker results would require careful analysis before attributing them to an event announced just days earlier.

## Frequently asked questions

### Has Jensen Huang left Nvidia?

Yes. Nvidia announced on **12 November 2026** that Huang resigned as president, CEO and a member of its board, effective immediately. The release does not announce any new executive role for him.

### Who replaced Jensen Huang as CEO?

**Colette Kress** is Nvidia's interim CEO and remains its chief financial officer. A permanent CEO has not yet been announced.

### Was Jensen Huang fired?

The official statement says **resigned**, not fired. Media accounts about the board's deliberations should not replace the precise wording of Nvidia's own announcement.

### Why is Nvidia stock falling after the CEO resignation?

Investors face questions about succession, governance and future execution. Short-term market moves can reflect changing expectations and liquidity as well as company fundamentals. Do not infer a precise cause or future price from one trading session.

### When is Nvidia's next earnings report?

**17 November 2026**, according to Nvidia, with results scheduled before its earnings conference call.

## The bottom line for Nvidia shareholders

Huang's immediate departure materially changes Nvidia's leadership structure, but it does **not automatically rewrite the company's near-term revenue or profit figures**. The essential investment test is whether the board and Kress can preserve execution while demonstrating credible governance and a durable succession plan.
