<section class="sol-article-grid"><article><h2>Where the quoted price goes</h2><p>Inspect the PV panels and mounting structure, inverter, isolators and protection, installation labour, roof access/scaffolding, any consumer-unit upgrades, monitoring, design and commissioning. A battery, hybrid inverter and backup-power features are not interchangeable extras.</p><p>Ask installers to state whether the quoted price is before or after any <a href="/solar/grants/">SEAI grant</a>, whether VAT is included and whether BER and electrical paperwork are included.</p></article><article><h2>Compare like with like</h2><p>Divide the <em>solar-only</em> installed price by array size in kWp to get a basic €/kWp comparison. Do not use this to rank packages with batteries or different roof works included. Compare predicted yield per kWp, product models, warranties, workmanship cover, exclusions and final scope.</p><p>For quote-specific analysis, use the <a href="/solar/compare-quotes/">solar quotation comparator</a>. For cost recovery after tariffs and grants, use the <a href="/solar-payback-calculator/">full payback calculator</a>.</p></article></section>
