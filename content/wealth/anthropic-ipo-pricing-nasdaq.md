Anthropic priced its initial public offering at **$83 a share** on Wednesday, 18 November, according to Bloomberg and *The Wall Street Journal*. That is **$5, or 6.4%, above** the top of the Claude maker’s preliminary **$70 to $78** range. The *Journal* also reported that Anthropic sold more shares than the **825 million** it had planned. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-18/anthropic-is-said-to-price-ipo-at-83-per-share) · [The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/finance/stocks/anthropic-prices-ipo-at-83-a-share-0290494c)

Anthropic has approval to list its Class A shares on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under **ANT**. Trading is expected to begin on Thursday, 19 November. Anthropic’s final prospectus was still pending when this article was updated. [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026074338/anthropicpbc-sx1ax3.htm) · [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/world-at-work/anthropic-ipo-mint-thousands-of-millionaires-2026-11-18/)

## How much did the IPO raise?

At $83, the original 825 million-share plan would have generated **$68.48 billion gross**, compared with **$64.35 billion** at the previous $78 ceiling. The reported increase in shares means the public deal would be larger than that $68.48 billion benchmark. Every additional million shares at $83 adds **$83 million** to gross proceeds. The final share count and the split between new shares and existing shareholders’ stock will determine how much goes to Anthropic itself. [Preliminary SEC prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026074338/anthropicpbc-sx1ax3.htm) · [The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/finance/stocks/anthropic-prices-ipo-at-83-a-share-0290494c)

The preliminary prospectus also describes a **$26 billion private placement** to strategic investors including Google and Nvidia. It is a separate sale of non-voting Class N shares at the IPO price, conditional on the IPO closing. The public offering includes shares sold by existing holders. Anthropic would receive proceeds from the shares it issues and from the private placement. [SEC filing, The Offering](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026074338/anthropicpbc-sx1ax3.htm)

## What does $83 imply for Anthropic’s valuation?

Before pricing, the *Financial Times* estimated that $83 a share would imply a valuation of about **$1.5 trillion**. The estimate values the whole company on a share-count basis that accounts for potential dilution. The precise calculation depends on which securities are included and the terms in the priced prospectus. [Financial Times](https://www.ft.com/content/0ef40ac9-6d01-428f-98c8-a52bf5b9a787)

## What Irish investors will see when ANT starts trading

Ireland is included in Anthropic’s European retail offer. The **IPO price** applies to shares allocated through the offering. Once Nasdaq trading starts, buyers and sellers establish a separate **opening trade price**, which can move above or below the offer. An Irish investor measuring their return in euro also has exposure to movements in EUR/USD. [European retail prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026073040/anthropicpbc-fwpxeeaprospea.htm)

The priced prospectus will give investors the final share count, underwriting fees and estimated net proceeds. Once ANT starts trading, the opening price will show how much buyers are willing to pay for the shares in the public market.
