Anthropic priced its initial public offering at **$83 a share**, Bloomberg reported on Wednesday, 18 November. That is **$5 above** the top of the Claude maker’s preliminary **$70 to $78** range. At the 825 million shares in the 17 November filing, the reported price implies a **$68.48 billion public offering** before fees. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-18/anthropic-is-said-to-price-ipo-at-83-per-share) · [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026074338/anthropicpbc-sx1ax3.htm)

Anthropic has approval to list its Class A shares on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under **ANT**. Trading is expected to begin on Thursday, 19 November. Anthropic’s final prospectus was still pending when checked, so the deal totals below use the latest preliminary share counts. [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026074338/anthropicpbc-sx1ax3.htm) · [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/world-at-work/anthropic-ipo-mint-thousands-of-millionaires-2026-11-18/)

## How the $68.48 billion offer breaks down

At $83, the proposed public offering would be **$4.13 billion larger** than at $78, the top of the filed range. Almost all of that increase would go to Anthropic because it is selling about 97% of the shares in the base deal. The rest would go to existing shareholders selling part of their stakes.

| Proposed sale | Shares | Gross at $78 | Gross at $83 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| New shares sold by Anthropic | 801,351,354 | $62.51bn | $66.51bn |
| Shares sold by existing owners | 23,648,646 | $1.84bn | $1.96bn |
| Total public offering | 825,000,000 | $64.35bn | $68.48bn |

*Compound calculations using the share counts in the 17 November SEC filing. Figures are in US dollars, rounded independently and before fees. The table excludes the underwriters’ option.*

The prospectus also describes a **$26 billion private placement** to strategic investors, including Google and Nvidia, conditional on the IPO closing. If both transactions closed on those terms at $83, Anthropic itself would receive about **$92.51 billion gross** from its own public shares and the private placement before fees or any extra shares sold through the underwriters’ option. [SEC filing, The Offering](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026074338/anthropicpbc-sx1ax3.htm)

## How does that compare with the $1.5 trillion valuation?

Before pricing, the *Financial Times* estimated that $83 a share would imply a valuation of about **$1.5 trillion**. The $68.48 billion figure measures the value of the shares being sold to the public. The $1.5 trillion estimate values the whole company on a share-count basis that accounts for potential dilution. The precise calculation depends on which securities are included and the terms in the priced prospectus. [Financial Times](https://www.ft.com/content/0ef40ac9-6d01-428f-98c8-a52bf5b9a787)

## What Irish investors will see when ANT starts trading

Ireland is included in Anthropic’s European retail offer. The **IPO price** applies to shares allocated through the offering. Once Nasdaq trading starts, buyers and sellers establish a separate **opening trade price**, which can move above or below the offer. An Irish investor measuring their return in euro also has exposure to movements in EUR/USD. [European retail prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026073040/anthropicpbc-fwpxeeaprospea.htm)

The priced prospectus will give investors the final share count, underwriting fees and estimated net proceeds. Once ANT starts trading, the opening price will show how much buyers are willing to pay for the shares in the public market.
