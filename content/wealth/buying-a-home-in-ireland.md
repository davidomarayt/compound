<h2 class="home-course-title">From your first savings target to the day you get the keys.</h2>

Buying a home is not one decision.

It is a chain of decisions that have to work together:

**budget → mortgage → supports → property → bid → survey → solicitor → drawdown → closing**

If one link is weak, the transaction can become stressful or expensive.

This nine-chapter course follows the Irish buying process in the order it actually happens.

## First-time buyer supports in 60 seconds

### Help to Buy

Revenue's enhanced Help to Buy scheme currently provides up to the lowest of:

- **€30,000**;
- **10%** of the qualifying new-home purchase price / self-build valuation;
- qualifying **Income Tax and DIRT paid over the relevant four previous tax years**.

The qualifying home must generally be a new build or self-build worth no more than **€500,000**, and the qualifying mortgage must meet the scheme's LTV rules.

### First Home Scheme

The First Home Scheme is shared equity.

It can provide up to:

- **30%** without Help to Buy;
- **20%** where Help to Buy is also used.

Price ceilings vary by local authority and property type.

Service charges start from year six, while the equity share itself remains linked to the value of the home.

Chapter 3 explains why that distinction matters.

## What the course is designed to prevent

The most expensive home-buying mistakes are rarely mathematical.

They are process mistakes.

Buying at the top of the bank's approval because "we can".

Bidding again because another €2,000 feels small.

Treating the lender valuation as a survey.

Signing contracts while a material question is unresolved.

Taking cashback without comparing the rate.

Using every euro at closing and starting home ownership with no reserve.

This course is designed to keep those decisions separate and deliberate.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much can a first-time buyer borrow in Ireland?

Under the standard Central Bank mortgage measures, first-time buyers are generally limited to four times gross income, while lenders still apply their own affordability assessment.

### How much deposit does a first-time buyer need?

The standard LTV rule requires at least a 10% deposit for a principal-home purchase, subject to the mortgage measures.

### What is Help to Buy worth?

Enhanced HTB can provide up to €30,000, but the actual claim is limited by 10% of the qualifying property value and the eligible Income Tax/DIRT you paid.

### Is the First Home Scheme a loan?

No. It is shared equity. The scheme takes a percentage interest in the home, and the euro cost of redeeming that percentage can change with the property's value.

### Is sale agreed legally binding?

For a normal private-treaty purchase, the transaction is generally not legally binding until contracts are signed through the conveyancing process.

### Do I need a structural survey?

It is not mandatory for every property, but the CCPC recommends survey/inspection particularly for second-hand and older homes. The lender's valuation is not a structural survey.
