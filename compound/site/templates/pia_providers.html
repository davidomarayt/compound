COST COMPARISON ENGINE

Compare costs on the same assumptions

When complete PIA-specific pricing is available, we'll compare provider fees for an identical investment balance and number of trades. Until then, the tool correctly displays an unavailable estimate rather than a guessed price.

Published fee models would include annual account, custody and reference-fund percentages plus fixed annual and per-trade charges. The estimate excludes FX costs, exit charges, investment tax, market movement and any fees not in the verified reference schedule. It is a simple one-year illustration, not a personalised quote.

How this comparison is compiled

We include providers with a directly attributable public statement about Ireland's planned scheme. All entries have a link to the organisation's own published information and are listed alphabetically. We do not rank or recommend providers while fees and product documents are unavailable. Changes must be verified against an official provider source before the tracker is updated.

Any paid referral or sponsorship is separately labelled and requires an approved partner agreement. Unpaid provider source links are not affiliate links. If sponsorship or affiliate relationships are added, they will be visibly labelled and editorial criteria will remain independent. Investment values can fall and returns are not guaranteed. This is general information, not personal investment advice.
