## What this calculator is for

Help to Buy is a Revenue tax-refund scheme for eligible first-time purchasers of qualifying new homes and self-builds. The amount available is constrained by both scheme limits and the qualifying Income Tax and DIRT actually paid.

## How to read the result

Treat the result as a planning estimate of possible support, not an approval. A buyer can satisfy the property rules but still receive less than the headline maximum if insufficient qualifying tax was paid.

## Worked example

If the scheme cap would allow €30,000 but the applicants have only €18,000 of qualifying Income Tax and DIRT available for refund, the tax paid becomes the binding limit. That is why the calculator needs both property and tax information.

## Irish context

HTB is not a general first-time-buyer grant for all homes. It is tied to qualifying new homes/self-builds and Revenue conditions. Eligibility, property-value limits, mortgage conditions and applicant status must all be confirmed through the Revenue process.

## What can change the answer

The support can change with the purchase price, qualifying tax history, number/status of applicants and whether the property/developer qualifies. Revenue can also apply clawback rules if scheme conditions are later breached.

## Common mistakes to avoid

Do not assume a couple automatically gets twice the maximum. Do not count HTB as guaranteed cash before Revenue confirmation. And do not confuse HTB with the First Home Scheme, which is shared equity rather than a tax refund.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use HTB on a second-hand home?
The scheme is aimed at qualifying new homes and self-builds; check Revenue's current rules.

### Is it a loan?
No. It is a tax refund subject to conditions.

### Can I rely on the calculator before Revenue approval?
No.

### Does it cover every first-time buyer?
No. Tax paid, property type, price and other conditions matter.

## Method and limitations

The tool applies the scheme limits encoded in the calculator to the values entered. It cannot validate Revenue records, buyer status, developer qualification, mortgage conditions or clawback exposure.
