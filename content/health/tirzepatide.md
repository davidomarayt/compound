**Mounjaro contains tirzepatide, a medicine that activates two hormone receptors called GIP and GLP-1.** It is given by weekly injection. Its EU authorisation covers type 2 diabetes and weight management in eligible adults, with criteria and prescribing instructions set out in the product information. [EMA Mounjaro overview](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/mounjaro) · [EMA product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/mounjaro-epar-product-information_en.pdf)

## The Science Behind the Drug

**GIP** stands for glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide. **GLP-1** stands for glucagon-like peptide-1. Both are hormones released by the gut in response to food. They are called **incretins** because they help the body release insulin when glucose is elevated. Tirzepatide is a single molecule that activates the receptors for both hormones. [EMA product information, section 5.1](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/mounjaro-epar-product-information_en.pdf)

Activation of these receptors affects insulin release and blood glucose. Tirzepatide also reduces appetite and food intake. The receptors are found in areas of the brain involved in appetite regulation, among other tissues. Clinical trials test the effects of the whole medicine, so the diagram shows its targets rather than how much each receptor contributes to the overall result. [EMA product information, section 5.1](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/mounjaro-epar-product-information_en.pdf)

## How does tirzepatide compare with semaglutide?

[Semaglutide](/health/medicines/semaglutide/) acts on the GLP-1 receptor. Tirzepatide acts on GLP-1 and GIP receptors. That distinction explains why tirzepatide is described as a **dual GIP and GLP-1 receptor agonist**. To compare clinical outcomes, look at studies that specify the dose, population and outcome measured. [EMA: Mounjaro](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/mounjaro-epar-product-information_en.pdf) · [EMA: Wegovy](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/wegovy-epar-product-information_en.pdf)

## What should you discuss with your prescriber?

Nausea, diarrhoea and vomiting can occur. Severe, persistent abdominal pain needs urgent medical advice as it can be a sign of pancreatitis. Tell your prescriber if you use insulin or a sulfonylurea, have significant digestive symptoms, or are pregnant or planning a pregnancy. Tell your procedure team if you are due to receive general anaesthesia or deep sedation. [EMA Mounjaro product information and leaflet](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/mounjaro-epar-product-information_en.pdf)

## What two receptor targets can and cannot tell you

GIP and GLP-1 are signals released after food intake. Tirzepatide can activate both receptors. That makes its mechanism different from semaglutide's, but receptor counts cannot predict how much weight any person will lose or how they will tolerate treatment. Outcomes have to be read from studies in the relevant population. A study in type 2 diabetes and a weight-management study may measure different outcomes and use different background treatment. [EMA Mounjaro information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/mounjaro-epar-product-information_en.pdf)

## Which information helps when a prescriber is reviewing you?

Bring why it was started, the exact product, any changes to the prescription and the timing of appetite or gastrointestinal symptoms. If you take insulin or another drug that can lower glucose, ask how the treatment plan deals with low readings. Tell the team about persistent vomiting or difficulty drinking, and about upcoming procedures involving deep sedation or anaesthesia. Severe persistent abdominal pain warrants urgent assessment. [EMA Mounjaro information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/mounjaro-epar-product-information_en.pdf)

If comparing with another product or changing treatment, check whether the plan refers to [semaglutide](/health/medicines/semaglutide/) or [liraglutide](/health/medicines/liraglutide/). These products have their own formulations, schedules and review points.
