## Palantir’s commercial business overtakes its US government business

**2 November 2026 · fiscal Q3 results.** A company long associated with government procurement now earns more US revenue from businesses. Commercial customers generated $984 million in the September quarter, $31 million more than US government customers. Commercial sales rose 148% year on year, and government sales themselves rose 96%. The crossover came because businesses bought software faster, even as government demand nearly doubled.

The US supplied roughly 84% of Palantir’s $2.31 billion in quarterly revenue. That concentration matters to anyone treating the result as evidence of a global rollout. The strongest evidence of adoption so far is domestic.

### Sales and new contracts tell different stories

US commercial contract value signed during the quarter grew 11% to $1.46 billion, well behind the 148% revenue growth. Signed contract value covers future work and depends on contract length. Revenue measures work recognised this quarter, including sales under earlier agreements. The gap alone cannot establish that demand has slowed. Remaining US commercial deal value offers a third reading: it rose 79% year on year to $6.52 billion but only 4% from June.

### What the next quarter needs to show

Palantir earned $1.08 billion in GAAP operating income, a 47% margin, and generated $1.20 billion in operating cash flow. Management projects $2.649 billion to $2.653 billion of fourth-quarter revenue. The midpoint requires roughly 15% sequential growth after a 20% increase this quarter. Investors can test that forecast against recognised sales and then check whether new contracts are replenishing future work fast enough to sustain the pace.
