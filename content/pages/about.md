Compound is an Irish site about three things that compound over time: your health, your wealth and your happiness. It is written for the person paying the rent, claiming the credit or trying to sleep better, not for the professional advising them.

## What we cover

**Wealth** is Irish money in practice: tax credits and reliefs people forget to claim, grants, pensions, renting and buying, bills and benefits. **Health** is sleep, movement, food, alcohol, sunlight, screens and the common conditions, told honestly, including where the evidence is thin. **Happiness** is relationships, work, money worry, habits, attention and loneliness, grounded in research rather than platitudes.

## How articles are made

Every piece starts with a question people in Ireland actually search for. Before a word is written, we gather the strongest sources we can find for it: official Irish guidance and statistics from bodies such as Revenue, Citizens Information, the HSE and the CSO, and, for health and happiness pieces, peer-reviewed research, with a preference for systematic reviews, meta-analyses and randomised trials.

Articles are researched and drafted with the help of AI tools, working only from those sources. Every number, date, threshold or study result in a piece must be quoted from its source, and each one is checked automatically against the text of that source before the piece can go live. A separate editorial pass scores each draft for accuracy, honesty about the evidence, usefulness and plain language, and sends it back if it falls short. Pieces that fail any of these checks are held for human review instead of being published. Every article lists its sources and shows the figures it uses, with a link to where each came from.

We name what a study is and how big it was, we say who a rule does not apply to, and we do not overstate. When a source is weak or sources disagree, the article says so.

## Corrections

If you spot a figure that is wrong or out of date, email [david@compound.ie](mailto:david@compound.ie) with the article link. Corrections are made promptly and noted on the piece. Accuracy is the point of the site.

## Independence

Compound is independent and is not affiliated with any government body, financial institution or health provider. Advertising on the site, where it appears, is served by third parties and has no influence on what we write.
