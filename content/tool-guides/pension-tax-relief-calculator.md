## What this calculator is for

This calculator estimates the amount of a personal pension contribution that may qualify for Irish Income Tax relief under the age-related percentage limits and the annual earnings cap.

## How to read the result

The key outputs are the **maximum contribution supported by the age/earnings limits**, the amount of your actual contribution that falls within that limit, and an illustrative Income Tax saving at the marginal tax rate you select.

## Worked example

A 42-year-old earning €40,000 falls in the 40–49 age band, where Revenue's limit is 25% of relevant earnings. That produces a maximum age-related amount of €10,000. A €6,000 contribution would therefore sit within that limit, subject to the other conditions.

## Irish context

Revenue's current age limits are 15% under 30, 20% at 30–39, 25% at 40–49, 30% at 50–54, 35% at 55–59 and 40% at 60+. The maximum earnings taken into account is €115,000 a year. Tax relief is Income Tax relief; employee pension contributions do not generally reduce USC or PRSI in the same way.

## What can change the answer

Age, relevant earnings, contribution amount and marginal Income Tax rate all affect the result. Employer contributions are treated differently from employee contributions and are not simply added into the same age-related limit in the way this tool models personal contributions.

## Common mistakes to avoid

Do not assume a 40% marginal rate means every euro of a contribution saves 40 cents if you do not have enough income taxed at 40%. Do not confuse a tax deduction with a cash bonus. And do not contribute money you may need before retirement simply for the relief.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does pension relief reduce USC and PRSI?
This calculator models Income Tax relief only.

### Is the €115,000 earnings cap the maximum I can contribute?
No. It is the maximum earnings base used for calculating the tax-relievable employee contribution limit.

### Do employer contributions use my age percentage?
Employer contribution rules differ; this tool is focused on the individual's contribution relief.

### Is relief automatic?
How relief is granted depends on the pension arrangement and payroll/tax process.

## Method and limitations

The tool caps relevant earnings at €115,000, applies the Revenue age-related percentage, then limits the eligible contribution to the lower of that amount and the contribution entered. The tax saving is an illustration at the chosen Income Tax rate.
