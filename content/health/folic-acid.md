## At a glance

Folic acid is a form of vitamin B9 used in settings such as folate deficiency and prevention of neural tube defects around pregnancy. The indication determines the dose and duration. [HSE guide](https://www2.hse.ie/medicines/folic-acid/)

## The Science Behind the Drug

### Folate metabolism, new cells and two distinct uses

Cells convert folic acid into folate forms used to transfer one-carbon units. These reactions help manufacture DNA building blocks and support production of new red blood cells. Folate also matters during early embryo development, when the neural tube forms. [HSE source](https://www2.hse.ie/medicines/folic-acid/)

## What the evidence measures

For deficiency, the clinical story and appropriate blood tests guide follow-up. For pregnancy prevention, timing around conception and individual risk matter. The evidence behind one use does not tell you which supplement regimen is appropriate for the other.

## Ask which use applies

| What to check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Pregnancy planning | ask when to start and whether your medical history changes the plan |
| Deficiency | ask how the cause and response will be assessed |


## Why does timing matter before pregnancy?

The neural tube forms early in development. Ask in advance about the timing and dose appropriate to your history, since the usual prevention regimen and management of higher risk are different questions.

## When to ask for help or a medication review

Doctors consider vitamin B12 deficiency when investigating anaemia. Folic acid can improve the blood findings even while B12-related neurological problems continue. Mention epilepsy medicines or methotrexate when seeking advice. [HSE patient guidance](https://www2.hse.ie/medicines/folic-acid/)
