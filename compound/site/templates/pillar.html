HOW WE BUILD HEALTH CONTENT

Evidence before certainty.

Health information can change decisions, so Compound separates estimates from diagnoses and explains the limits of each tool. Irish guidance is prioritised where it exists, with sources such as the HSE and HPRA alongside peer-reviewed evidence.

Medication pages explain authorised uses, access and safety rather than recommending treatment. Urgent symptoms belong with emergency services or a clinician, not a calculator.
