**Ireland's Employment Permits Portal remains unavailable. The Department has begun testing a temporary alternative application process, but only with a limited number of companies; it is not yet a general replacement route for every applicant.**

That distinction is the important part of the [Department's 19 November service update](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/employment-permits-portal-service/employment-permits-portal-service.html). The temporary process is a partial recovery measure while investigation, remediation and security testing continue. No date has been announced for the portal to reopen.

Applications already received continue to be processed. People preparing new applications, renewals or reviews need to follow different steps rather than treating the outage as an automatic extension.

## What each applicant group can do

| Your position | Current official guidance |
| --- | --- |
| Application already submitted | It remains in processing; do not create a duplicate solely because of the outage |
| Draft saved in the portal | The Department expects drafts to remain available; retain your documents and wait for further instructions |
| New application not submitted | General email submission is not currently available; prepare documents and monitor the official update page |
| Permit renewal approaching | Keep the renewal documents ready, but do not assume the permit or immigration permission is automatically extended |
| Review or appeal deadline running | The 28-day deadline still applies; submit the prescribed form by email to EPreviews@enterprise.gov.ie |
| Labour Market Needs Test underway | Keep evidence of publication dates and advertising records |

The Department says flexibility will be applied so applicants are not disadvantaged by the disruption. Its FAQ specifically says drafts affected by the normal 28-day period will be treated flexibly. That is not the same as a blanket extension of a permit, immigration permission or appeal deadline.

## The temporary process is still limited

Testing with a small number of companies is meant to provide a secure route for new applications while the main system is restored. The Department says it is working to extend access and will publish more details.

Until that happens, an employer or agent outside the test should not send a full new application to a general email address and assume it has been lodged. The official FAQ says new applications cannot currently be submitted by email.

For an urgent application query, the Department lists **epos@enterprise.gov.ie**. Applicants should avoid sending unnecessary identity documents in an initial query and should follow any secure submission instructions provided directly by the Department.

## Processing dates are a queue snapshot, not a promise

The Department's [processing-date page](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/current-application-processing-dates/) showed the following on 20 November 2026:

| Application stream | Processing applications received on |
| --- | ---: |
| Critical Skills — new | 23 October 2026 |
| General Employment Permit — new | 23 October 2026 |
| Other new applications | 23 October 2026 |
| Intra-Company Transfer — new | 22 October 2026 |
| Intra-Company Transfer — renewal | 16 October 2026 |
| Other renewals | 14 October 2026 |
| Reviews and appeals | 6 May 2026 |

These dates identify the receipt dates then being processed. They are not guaranteed completion dates and can move at different speeds between streams.

For a worker whose start date or continued employment depends on a permit, that uncertainty has a direct household-finance consequence. Employers and employees should document any revised start date, unpaid period or contingency arrangement rather than relying on an assumed portal recovery date.

## What the Department says happened to personal data

The portal was taken offline after suspicious unauthorised activity was identified on elements of the system. The Department's [security-incident notice](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/publications/employment-permits-portal-personal-data-security-incident.html) says first and last names were downloaded for the general customer population. It says email addresses and account roles were not downloaded in that general dataset.

A very small number of people whose records were separately investigated were contacted directly, according to the Department. Anyone with a data question can use **dataqueries.permits@enterprise.gov.ie**; the Data Protection Officer contact is **dataprotection@enterprise.gov.ie**.

This article does not infer what caused the activity or who was responsible. The official investigation is continuing.

## A practical checklist while the portal is unavailable

1. Save the official service-update and FAQ links rather than relying on social-media screenshots.
2. Keep dated copies of passport, contract, qualification and employer documents required for the intended permit type.
3. Preserve screenshots or reference numbers for submitted applications and drafts.
4. Diary any appeal, review, permission or employment-start deadline separately.
5. If a Labour Market Needs Test is required, retain the advertisement and proof of its publication period.
6. Agree in writing how the employer will handle a delayed start or renewal.
7. Use the official contact address for an urgent case, but do not submit a duplicate application unless instructed.

The 19 November update shows that the service is moving beyond investigation into a limited alternative process. For most users, however, the actionable message remains cautious: prepare, preserve deadlines and records, and wait for the Department to confirm a submission route that applies to your case.
