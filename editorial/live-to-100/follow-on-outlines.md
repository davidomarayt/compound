## Part 2 — Wealth for a 100-Year Life

- **Reader promise:** Understand how a potentially long later life changes the questions to ask about income, housing and financial flexibility in Ireland.
- **Pillar:** Wealth. **Series order:** 2. **Status:** Full article completed for publication.
- **Proposed route:** `/wealth/wealth-for-a-100-year-life/`.
- **SEO title:** Wealth for a 100-Year Life in Ireland | Compound.
- **Meta description:** Plan for a potentially longer life in Ireland: pensions, inflation, housing, care and changing work, with uncertainty made clear.

Principal sections:

1. What income would need to cover: essential costs, choices and uneven spending over time.
2. Retirement date versus pension access: State Pension, work pensions and personal pensions, with the current Irish rules independently checked.
3. Prices and uncertainty: nominal versus real spending, fees, taxes, investment risk and uncertain returns. Illustrations must state assumptions and show alternative outcomes.
4. Housing: renting, ownership, maintenance, accessibility and relocation choices, without treating one path as universal.
5. Care and family support: current Irish schemes, eligibility, cost variability and the limits of informal care.
6. Work across a longer life: retraining, breaks and phased retirement where feasible.
7. Inheritance, decision support and administration: introduce the relevant Irish processes without personalised legal advice.
8. A practical review of the reader’s current position and questions for an appropriately qualified adviser.

**Already covered in Part 1:** the 100-year scenario, the simple retirement-duration subtraction, basic inflation definition and why money connects with health and relationships. Link back briefly rather than repeating that introduction.

**Existing content to connect:** `/compound-interest-calculator/`, `/wealth/pension-tax-relief-ireland-how-to-claim/`, `/wealth/mortgage-overpayments-100-euro-ireland/`. Recheck current content and rules before linking from the finished article.

## Part 3 — Health for a 100-Year Life

- **Reader promise:** Prioritise evidence-based ways to support health, capability and participation while separating established advice from longevity marketing.
- **Pillar:** Health. **Series order:** 3. **Status:** Full article completed for publication.
- **Proposed route:** `/health/health-for-a-100-year-life/`.
- **SEO title:** Health for a 100-Year Life in Ireland | Compound.
- **Meta description:** An Irish guide to healthspan, strength, heart and brain health, nutrition, sleep and prevention, with realistic expectations for later life.

Principal sections:

1. Turn “healthspan” into meaningful capabilities and personal priorities, including life with disability or chronic illness.
2. Movement, strength and balance: current Irish guidance, adaptable starting points and where individual advice matters.
3. Cardiovascular and metabolic health: established prevention, risk assessment and the role of clinical care; no universal testing bundle.
4. Food and nutrition: overall dietary patterns, later-life needs and gaps that warrant qualified advice. Avoid duplicating the nutrient encyclopedia.
5. Sleep, tobacco and alcohol: supported measures, practical constraints and clear evidence limits.
6. Brain, hearing and vision: distinguish risk reduction from guaranteed prevention; evaluate the underlying human evidence.
7. Vaccination, screening and routine care: current HSE programmes and eligibility, with symptoms directed to clinical assessment.
8. Maintaining independence with support: environment, aids, rehabilitation and access to care.
9. Assessing supplements, tests and “anti-ageing” claims: what outcomes were actually measured and in whom?

**Already covered in Part 1:** lifespan versus healthspan, healthy-life-years caveats, the value of support, and a general rationale for prevention. Do not repeat the demographic opening or promise a centenarian outcome.

**Existing content to connect:** `/health/macronutrients-micronutrients-guide-ireland/`, `/health/how-much-sleep-do-adults-need/`, `/health/lower-blood-pressure-naturally-what-works/`, `/bmi-calculator/`. Review for accuracy and overlap before writing.

## Part 4 — Happiness for a 100-Year Life

- **Reader promise:** Explore ways to sustain belonging, meaning and enjoyable daily life through changing relationships, work, health and responsibilities.
- **Pillar:** Happiness. **Series order:** 4. **Status:** Full article completed for publication.
- **Proposed route:** `/happiness/happiness-for-a-100-year-life/`.
- **SEO title:** Happiness for a 100-Year Life in Ireland | Compound.
- **Meta description:** Explore friendship, community, purpose and changing identity through a longer life, with practical Irish context and careful use of research.

Principal sections:

1. An ordinary worthwhile day: values and enjoyment without compulsory positivity or constant productivity.
2. Connection: distinguish loneliness, isolation and solitude; avoid assuming marriage, children or reliable family support.
3. Keeping and making relationships across transitions, with realistic attention to disability, transport, cost and time.
4. Community in Ireland: accessible examples of groups, libraries, volunteering and cultural participation, with current availability verified.
5. Work and retirement identity: what changes, what may be missed and what can be carried forward.
6. Curiosity, learning, play and manageable commitments; distinguish observational research from intervention evidence.
7. Bereavement, caring and adaptation: acknowledge difficulty without presenting hobbies as treatment.
8. When more support is needed: current Irish mental-health and community support routes, carefully checked.
9. A short reflection on relationships, enjoyable activities and realistic next steps.

**Already covered in Part 1:** why connection and purpose deserve a place in a longer-life plan, the limits of mortality associations and the ordinary-day question. Part 4 develops the practical and psychological questions.
