02 / HOW TO READ THIS DATABASE

Only count what can be counted once.

Different financial scopes

A sector-wide estimate may include many departmental programmes. A programme amount can in turn contain smaller sub-programmes. The entries above are not an additive breakdown of all government spending.

Budgets versus spending

Most entries are 2026 estimates or allocations, not audited spending. Some represent additional Budget measures, and central charges are projections. Each page identifies its own basis.

Why amounts may overlap

Housing current and capital envelopes are parts of its programme total, while Child Benefit is included within broader family payments. For these cases, individual pages explain the connection.

Original charts and sources

Compound builds the charts locally from the source-linked data. Official websites are cited for verification, rather than being used as the destination for an interactive chart.
