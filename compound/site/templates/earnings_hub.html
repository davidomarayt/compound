HOW IT WORKS

Original figures. Clear limitations.

These articles are generated from SEC XBRL financial-statement data, not analyst forecasts. A quarter is only reported if standalone three-month figures are available. A 10-K is treated as a full-year report, not as a fourth-quarter release.

Some companies' earnings press releases appear days or weeks before their 10-Q or 10-K filings; this feed is not a real-time earnings-release wire. Reports are deterministic and automatically source-checked, but they are not individually reviewed before publication. Corrections: david@compound.ie.

These results do not represent personalised investment advice or imply that any shares can be bought in a PIA. Read the official filing before making decisions.
