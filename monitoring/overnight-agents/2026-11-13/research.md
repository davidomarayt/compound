## 5. Original Research Agent — Ireland September 2026 new mortgage rates; two non-equivalent comparisons

**Original primary dataset:** [Central Bank of Ireland, Retail Interest Rates – September 2026](https://www.centralbank.ie/statistics/data-and-analysis/credit-and-banking-statistics/retail-interest-rates), under Household Lending Interest Rates, observed September **2026**, page verified **13 November 2026**. The page labels the data period but does not establish a separate first-publication date in the material inspected; do **not** invent a release timestamp. The methodology describes collection from all Irish-resident banks from November 2025 and an August 2026 methodological change affecting data since November 2025, so careless long-run comparisons are unsafe.

**Source figures and units:**
- Weighted average interest rate on **new Irish mortgage agreements**, September 2026: **3.52% per annum**. Down **1 basis point** (0.01 percentage points) from August and down **7 basis points** year over year.
- Euro-area comparator: **3.64%**, giving **0.12 percentage points = 12 basis points** above the Irish average. The source reports Ireland as the euro area's **12th highest**, not lowest.
- **Fixed-rate new mortgage agreements:** **3.47%**, with fixed products accounting for **94% of the *volume* of new agreements**. This is not “94% of mortgage borrowers” or “94% of existing mortgages.”
- **Variable-rate new mortgage agreements:** **4.30%**, up 14 basis points from August. These are aggregates on different borrower/product mixes, not two offers for the same person.
- Monthly volume of new mortgage agreements: **€1.4 billion**; **€103 million** (8%) above August. This is **agreement volume**, not directly proof of cash drawn down or home prices.
- New household deposits with agreed maturity averaged **2.03%**; household overnight deposits **0.23%**. Different deposit terms, not comparable guaranteed personal offers.

**Reproducible original calculation (illustrative, not a rate quote):** For a fictional **€300,000** fully amortising loan over **25 years = 300 monthly payments**, with nominal annual rate `i` and monthly rate `r=i/12`, monthly payment `M=P*r/[1-(1+r)^(-300)]`. At **3.52%**, `M=€1,505.09/month`; at **3.64%**, `M=€1,524.49/month`; difference **€19.40/month**, or **€232.79 for 12 identical payments** if those assumptions held. Formula excludes fees, changing rates, approval/credit factors, cashback, repayment conventions and all tax effects. The **12-bp national-average difference is *not* a predicted saving for a particular borrower**; both averages describe different samples. As a rate context only, the September fixed/variable group averages are 3.47%/4.30%, but their 83-bp difference is **not** a controlled matched-loan comparison.
