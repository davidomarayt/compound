## What this calculator is for

The First Home Scheme is a shared-equity support. It is intended to help eligible buyers bridge a gap between their deposit plus mortgage and the price of a qualifying home. The scheme receives an equity share rather than giving an ordinary unsecured loan.

## How to read the result

Focus on the **equity percentage**, not only the euro contribution. If the property value changes, the cost of redeeming that share can change too. The calculator is therefore best used to understand the funding structure, not to predict a future buyout price.

## Worked example

If a €400,000 home has a €40,000 deposit and a €320,000 mortgage, the funding gap is €40,000. A €40,000 equity contribution would represent 10% of that purchase price at outset, subject to scheme eligibility and limits.

## Irish context

The scheme has eligibility conditions, participating lenders and property-price ceilings that can vary by location/property type. Service charges can also apply under the scheme terms after specified periods. Always check the live scheme documentation before relying on the result.

## What can change the answer

Future property value affects the euro value of a percentage equity share. Service charges, redemption timing and whether you buy out part or all of the share also alter the economics.

## Common mistakes to avoid

Do not treat shared equity as a grant. Do not assume the initial euro amount is the future redemption cost. And do not use the calculator as proof of scheme eligibility or mortgage approval.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the equity contribution a grant?
No.

### Does it guarantee I can buy the property?
No. Mortgage and scheme eligibility still matter.

### Can the buyout cost rise?
Yes, if the relevant property value rises.

### Is it the same as Help to Buy?
No. HTB is a tax-refund scheme.

## Method and limitations

The calculator models the funding gap and the selected shared-equity percentage. It does not determine formal eligibility, future property value, service charges, redemption cost or lender approval.
