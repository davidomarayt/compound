What to understand

The 2025 comparator is a supplementary estimate, not an audited full-year outturn. The 2026 amount is a revised budget estimate. A difference between these two does not by itself establish a change in eligibility or the outcome of services.

The original source does not establish a payment to an individual farmer, organisation or household.
