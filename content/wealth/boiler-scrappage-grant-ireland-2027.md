<p><strong>The €2,000 boiler scrappage grant is no longer just a proposal.</strong> SEAI now includes it within the revised heat-pump support scheme, which opened from 6 October 2026. The maximum combined support for qualifying houses with a water-based heat pump is <strong>€14,500</strong>, subject to all individual criteria.</p>

When this article was first published on **23 September 2026**, the Minister had proposed a €2,000 payment for scrapping oil or kerosene boilers. The proposal has since been incorporated into the [SEAI heat-pump grant scheme](https://www.seai.ie/grants/home-energy-grants/individual-grants/heat-pump-systems). Earlier references on this page to awaiting Cabinet approval or a January 2027 launch are now superseded.

## What is confirmed in October 2026?

Under the current individual grant route, the potential combined support for qualifying **detached, semi-detached and terraced houses** installing a water-based heat pump is:

| Component | Maximum amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Heat Pump Unit Grant | €10,500 |
| Central-heating upgrade grant, where required | €2,000 |
| Boiler Scrappage Grant, where eligible | €2,000 |
| **Total possible grant** | **€14,500** |

The €2,000 scrappage element can apply when an eligible **oil or gas boiler, solid-fuel or electric storage-heating system** is removed. SEAI also describes eligibility for homeowners without existing central heating who are installing a heat pump. The central-heating grant is **not automatic**: the contractor's design must show that qualifying radiator or underfloor-heating work is required.

SEAI separately publishes up to **€11,500 for apartments** with qualifying water-based heat pumps and up to **€9,500 for air-to-air** systems. Grants cannot exceed the cost of eligible works. Replacement of an existing heat pump has separate limits and generally cannot attract the scrappage and central-heating extras.

## Who can apply?

For the individual Better Energy Homes route, the home generally must have been **built and occupied before 2021**, with an MPRN and a registered contractor. Owner-occupiers, eligible landlords and certain other property-owning organisations can apply.

Homes built before 2007 may require a heat-loss technical assessment unless a valid BER already shows a suitable HLI. Up to €200 is available for an eligible technical assessment. **Apply and receive SEAI's grant offer before starting the work.**

## Does the grant make replacing oil worthwhile?

It can improve the upfront calculation, but lifetime economics still depend on the homeowner's **actual oil price, electricity tariff, boiler efficiency, heat demand and achieved heat-pump SCOP**.

Our newer article, **[New €14,500 Heat-Pump Grant: Is Switching From Oil Worth It?](/news/heat-pump-grant-14500-oil-vs-heat-pump-ireland/)**, includes two transparent charts showing hypothetical running costs and how varying grant eligibility affects the net installation bill.

For a broader project, use the [Whole-House Retrofit Planner](/whole-house-retrofit-planner/). If the planned heat pump increases daytime electricity demand, you can independently test [solar payback](/solar-payback-calculator/) and read the [Solar Hub](/solar/) — but solar cannot be assumed to cover winter heating demand.

**Bottom line:** The originally proposed payment is now a confirmed SEAI grant component. Do not use old September reporting or assume all households automatically get the maximum amount. Check your eligibility against the [current SEAI rules](https://www.seai.ie/grants/home-energy-grants/individual-grants/heat-pump-systems) before paying a deposit.
