## What this calculator is for

A whole-house retrofit is a sequence of interacting measures rather than a shopping list. This planner helps assemble costs, grants and estimated energy effects across insulation, heating, ventilation, windows, solar and related upgrades.

## How to read the result

The cheapest measure is not always the first measure you should do, but fabric measures often influence the suitability and sizing of later heating upgrades. Focus on the post-grant cost, expected comfort/energy effect and the dependencies between measures.

## Worked example

Improving insulation and airtightness can reduce heat demand before a heat pump is sized. Installing a heat pump first in a poorly performing home can lead to a system that must work harder and may require expensive distribution upgrades later.

## Irish context

SEAI offers individual grants and a One Stop Shop route for complete home upgrades. One Stop Shop projects generally target a minimum B BER and can include project management, assessment and multiple grant-supported measures. Grant values and eligibility differ by property type.

## What can change the answer

Home age, construction type, existing BER, heating system, moisture risk, ventilation, contractor quotes and grant eligibility all matter. Traditional pre-1940 buildings can require different fabric approaches.

## Common mistakes to avoid

Do not chase a BER letter while ignoring moisture, ventilation or comfort. Do not start grant-funded works before required approval. Do not assume a grant makes a measure economical without comparing the net cost. And avoid replacing windows purely for payback when cheaper fabric measures have not been assessed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a One Stop Shop mandatory?
No. SEAI also provides individual-grant routes.

### Should I insulate before installing a heat pump?
The whole-house design should consider fabric and heat demand before final heat-pump sizing.

### Are grant values the same for every property?
No.

### Does this replace a home energy assessment?
No.

## Method and limitations

The planner combines the selected measures, entered costs and implemented grant assumptions into a scenario. Energy savings are indicative and cannot reproduce a detailed DEAP assessment or contractor design.
