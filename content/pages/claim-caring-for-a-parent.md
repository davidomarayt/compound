If your parent needs more help at home, start by arranging a **HSE Home Support assessment** and writing down the visits they need in a typical week. Then check whether you qualify for a carer payment and price any visits you will have to arrange privately. If the plan starts to depend on overnight or unpredictable care that nobody can reliably provide, discuss the options with their care team.

## Apply for HSE Home Support {#home-support}

The HSE’s [Home Support Service](https://www2.hse.ie/services/home-support-service/home-support-service-for-older-people/) can help with everyday personal care, such as washing, dressing and getting in or out of bed. It is generally for people aged 65 or over, with some exceptions for younger people. HSE-provided support is free, with no income test or medical card requirement. The HSE assesses your parent’s needs and tells them what it can provide.

- **If your parent is at home**, use the [official form or contact the local Home Support Office](https://www.hse.ie/services/home-support-service/how-to-apply/).
- **If your parent is in hospital**, ask the person managing discharge for the application form. Explain what family can reliably cover, including evenings and weekends.
- **For the assessment**, write down what help your parent needs, which days and times are hardest, what family already does and what your parent would prefer. The [HSE assessment guide](https://www.hse.ie/services/home-support-service/after-you-apply/) explains what it considers.

After the decision, ask which visits are confirmed, when they start and how any missed visit will be covered. Base the private care budget on the confirmed schedule.

## Check payments for family carers {#carer-payments}

| Support | When to check it | Where to apply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Carer’s Allowance** | You provide full-time care and attention and want to check the household means test and work or study conditions. | [Department of Social Protection](https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-social-protection/services/carers-allowance/) |
| **Carer’s Benefit** | You are leaving work or reducing your hours to provide full-time care and may meet the PRSI and employment conditions. Ask your employer about Carer’s Leave separately. | [Department of Social Protection](https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-social-protection/services/carers-benefit/) |
| **Carer’s Support Grant** | You receive an eligible carer payment, or you provide full-time care and want to check the criteria for applying separately. This is an annual payment. | [Department of Social Protection](https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-social-protection/services/carers-support-grant/) |

Keep a note of your work or study hours, household income where relevant and your parent’s care needs. The scheme pages list the forms and medical evidence to check. In your budget, record any confirmed carer payment and income lost from reducing work separately from your parent’s care bill.

## Worked weekly home care budget {#weekly-budget}

Suppose you and your parent identify **16 hours of scheduled help a week**. Family can reliably cover four, the HSE confirms six, and a private provider quotes **€30 per billable hour** for the remaining six. The rate and hours are illustrative, so substitute the actual visit schedule and written provider quote.

| Weekly cover | Hours | Direct private care bill |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Family visits | 4 | €0 |
| Confirmed HSE visits | 6 | €0 |
| Private visits at €30 per hour | 6 | €180 |
| **Total** | **16** | **€180** |

If the private visits run for 52 weeks, that is **€9,360 a year**, or **€780 per month** on average. If you need private visits for the six HSE hours while waiting for them to start, 12 hours at the same example rate would cost **€360 a week** until they begin.

Ask providers about minimum visit length, weekend rates, cancellation terms and replacement cover. Add any lost take-home pay or other costs separately. <a href="/home-care-cost-calculator/" data-claim-tool="home-care-cost">Calculate your home care budget</a> using your own figures.

## If nursing home care is being considered {#fair-deal}

[Apply for Fair Deal through the HSE](https://www.hse.ie/services/schemes-allowances/fair-deal-scheme/application-process/) if your parent may need long-term nursing home care. The HSE assesses care needs and works out the applicant’s contribution from assessable income and assets. For approved care, the HSE pays the balance of the scheme-covered cost. Ask the home for its fees and any separately charged services in writing, and confirm the funding start date before committing to a place.

Bring together pension and bank statements, property details and evidence of allowable expenses for the [financial assessment](https://www2.hse.ie/services/schemes-allowances/fair-deal-scheme/financial-assessment/). The HSE explains the three-year cap on assessing the principal home and, separately, the [optional nursing home loan](https://www.hse.ie/services/schemes-allowances/fair-deal-scheme/nursing-home-loan/), which can defer an eligible property-based payment.
