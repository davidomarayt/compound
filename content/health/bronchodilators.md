## At a glance

Bronchodilators widen airways by reducing constriction. They include reliever and maintenance treatments with different roles in asthma and COPD. [HSE guide](https://www2.hse.ie/medicines/bronchodilators/)

## The Science Behind the Drug

### Relaxing the airway by two different routes

Beta-2 agonists increase a cell signal called cyclic AMP in airway smooth muscle, helping the muscle relax. Antimuscarinics reduce the constricting action of acetylcholine at airway muscarinic receptors. Both mechanisms can improve airflow. Inhaled delivery depends on matching the inhaler device and breathing technique. [HSE source](https://www2.hse.ie/medicines/bronchodilators/)

## What the evidence measures

Quick symptom relief, changes in spirometry and prevention of flare-ups are separate outcomes. A device demonstration can reveal an inhalation issue that looks like a medicine failure.

## Know the role of each device

| What to check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Reliever | confirm the role in the personal action plan |
| Maintenance | ask what the regular inhaler is intended to prevent |


## Why can two inhalers both widen the airway yet serve different roles?

Their onset, duration and additional ingredients can differ. The decision about which inhaler to use and when comes from the individual treatment plan and the exact device.

## When to ask for help or a medication review

Follow the prescribed asthma or COPD plan. New or worsening severe breathlessness or failure of the emergency plan needs urgent help. A pharmacist or respiratory nurse can check how the device is being used. [HSE patient guidance](https://www2.hse.ie/medicines/bronchodilators/)
