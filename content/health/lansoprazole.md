## At a glance

Lansoprazole is a proton pump inhibitor used in acid-related conditions. Formulation and treatment duration should match the reason for use and the product supplied. [HSE guide](https://www2.hse.ie/medicines/lansoprazole/)

## The Science Behind the Drug

### Acid pump blockade and why formulations matter

Lansoprazole reaches the gastric parietal cell, where an acidic compartment activates it. The active form inhibits the hydrogen-potassium ATPase in acid-secreting cells. This suppresses new acid production. Gastro-resistant formulations protect the medicine during transit through the stomach before it can be absorbed. [DailyMed source](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=18b4020f-d7e6-42f8-838d-2b5da152530e)

## What the evidence measures

Reflux symptoms may change over a different timeframe from ulcer healing. Some prescriptions are part of a wider strategy for an infection or protection against another medicine. Check what the treatment is intended to achieve.

## Understand a formulation switch

| What to check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Capsule or dispersible tablet | check the package instructions for that product |
| Temporary or ongoing use | confirm the reason and review date |


## Why do some products have a gastro-resistant label?

The formulation protects the drug during passage through the acidic stomach before absorption. Follow the pack instructions or ask a pharmacist before crushing or changing the dosage form.

## When to ask for help or a medication review

Seek assessment for difficulty swallowing, bleeding symptoms or unexplained weight loss. Ask a pharmacist how your formulation should be used if swallowing is difficult. [HSE patient guidance](https://www2.hse.ie/medicines/lansoprazole/)
