Nvidia shareholders face a fresh question about the company’s leadership after Reuters relayed a New York Times report alleging that Jensen Huang had a relationship with Kimberly Powell, Nvidia’s vice president of healthcare, who reported to him. The Times cited five people who had worked at the company. Nvidia had not immediately responded to Reuters’ request for comment. **The allegation has not been independently verified by Compound.** [Reuters report](https://www.reuters.com/business/nvidia-ceo-huang-had-relationship-with-executive-who-reported-him-nyt-reports-2026-11-11/)

For investors, the issue is how the board handles possible conflicts, leadership continuity and accountability. Those questions can affect the price shareholders will pay for future profits, even before there is evidence of a change in chip demand.

## What Nvidia has confirmed

On 8 November, Huang acknowledged marital infidelity in an [email published by Nvidia](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/a-message-to-our-company). He also said he would cooperate with the independent investigation into national-security concerns raised in news reports. That message did not name Powell and should not be treated as confirmation of the new allegation.

In its [separate statement that day](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/statement-from-nvidia-board-of-directors), the board announced an investigation into reports that Huang had been targeted by a foreign intelligence effort. It said it had no reason to believe national security or company secrets had been compromised and expressed confidence in Huang and his leadership team. Those were the board’s statements on 8 November, not findings from a completed investigation.

On 10 November, the [independent directors confirmed](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/Statement-from-the-Independent-Members-of-the-NVIDIA-Board/default.aspx) that they had retained Joon H. Kim and Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz to conduct the inquiry. These announcements establish that an investigation exists; they do not establish that the allegations are proven.

## Reports about Huang’s future are a separate development

Later on 11 November, Reuters carried a Wall Street Journal report that Nvidia was preparing to remove Huang. Reuters said it could not independently verify the reports, and Nvidia had not immediately commented. A reported plan is **not a confirmed departure or an announced successor**. [Reuters leadership report](https://www.reuters.com/technology/nvidia-is-preparing-oust-ceo-jensen-huang-wsj-reports-2026-11-11/)

This article reflects the sources available on 11 November. Readers should check subsequent company announcements before treating the leadership position as settled.

## How governance uncertainty reaches a share price

There are two different ways the investment case could change. The first is operational: disruption might eventually affect customer relationships, product execution or financial performance. That requires evidence. An allegation alone does not demonstrate that orders have been cancelled or earnings forecasts need to fall.

The second is valuation. Investors may demand a lower price for the same expected earnings when they become less confident about oversight or succession. As a purely hypothetical example, earnings of $5 a share valued at 30 times earnings imply a $150 share price. At 27 times the same earnings, the price becomes $135: a 10% decline with no change in the earnings assumption. These figures are illustrative, not Nvidia forecasts or price targets.

A credible board response could reduce that uncertainty. A poorly explained transition or unresolved questions could prolong it. Neither outcome can be inferred from a viral post.

## The evidence shareholders should watch

| Development | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| A formal board announcement on leadership | Establishes whether responsibilities have changed and who is accountable for execution. |
| The investigation’s scope and eventual findings | Helps distinguish allegations from established facts and identify any control failures. |
| A clear continuity or succession plan | Lets investors assess how strategy and customer relationships would be managed through a transition. |
| Subsequent results and management guidance | Provides evidence about the business, rather than assuming reputational pressure has already changed revenue. |

Several differently named investments can still depend on the same company. The relevant calculation is the direct holding plus each fund holding multiplied by that fund’s Nvidia weight, using current fund disclosures.
