    07 · TRUST AND METHODOLOGY

    Know which numbers are certain.

    We separate exact formula calculations, rounded public spending estimates, and exploratory topics that cannot yet be reliably priced.

    Calculated

    PAYE income tax, USC and employee Class A PRSI use the displayed 2026 assumptions. Results are rounded for presentation, not within each formula.

    Estimated

    Sector totals are the government's 2026 published estimates, rounded to €0.1bn. Personal sector amounts are illustrative: your payroll taxes × sector spending ÷ total spending.

    Not a net-benefit score

    Public goods, healthcare protection, education and government services do not all have a defensible household cash value. We do not invent one.

    Limitations

    No self-employment, non-PAYE income, age-specific credits, home-carer credit, unusual PRSI classes, BIK, capital taxes, full indirect taxes or pension-relief limit validation. Individual circumstances differ.
