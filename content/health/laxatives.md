## At a glance

Laxatives are used for constipation and certain other indications. How they work and how rapidly they act depend on the active ingredient and route. [HSE guide](https://www2.hse.ie/medicines/laxatives/)

## The Science Behind the Drug

### Water, fibre, bowel movement and differences between types

Bulk-forming products hold water and increase stool bulk. Osmotic laxatives retain more water in the bowel. Stimulants act on bowel movement and secretion. Each step changes a different property of bowel function, which is why the same product may not fit every pattern of constipation. [HSE source](https://www2.hse.ie/medicines/laxatives/)

## What the evidence measures

A useful outcome is easier bowel movements with less straining, not simply a higher count. New symptoms, effects of other medicines and duration of constipation all inform the review.

## Describe the problem before picking a product

| What to check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Stool | record hardness, consistency and frequency |
| Symptoms | note straining, pain, bloating and recent medicine changes |


## What is worth recording besides the number of bowel movements?

Note stool consistency, straining, discomfort and any recent medicine changes. The same frequency can describe very different problems.

## When to ask for help or a medication review

Obtain assessment for severe pain with vomiting, abdominal swelling or an inability to pass stool or gas. Ask about fluid requirements and whether an underlying cause or medicine needs review. [HSE patient guidance](https://www2.hse.ie/medicines/laxatives/)
