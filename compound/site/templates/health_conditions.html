How to read the Irish figures

A prevalence estimate describes how many people in a defined group have a condition or characteristic. A notification counts a report to a designated authority. Screening, vaccination and treatment figures describe the activity or people recorded by the relevant programme.

Each figure gives the population, period and original source. A missing figure means Compound has yet to verify one for this entry. The HSE index also includes symptoms, tests, treatments and services, which may have a different type of data.
