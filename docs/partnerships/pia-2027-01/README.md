## What a four-week pilot could include

A fixed-fee advertisement on the PIA hub with the provider’s name, a short factual message and a link to its official information page. The closing report would confirm the placement and dates, with aggregate clicks where measurement permits.

The panel would carry a visible paid label. Compound chooses the sources, sets calculator assumptions and applies the same comparison criteria to every provider. A sponsor may verify the wording of its own advertisement.
