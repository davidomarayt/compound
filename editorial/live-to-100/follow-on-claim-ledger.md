## Editorial boundaries

No longevity probability, demographic projection, personal pension entitlement, investment return, treatment prescription or promised happiness improvement is calculated. Numerical rules retain their population, period and meaning. Practical prompts are not validated clinical scales. Original images are explicitly credited as AI illustrations. No independent clinical or financial specialist review is claimed.

Earlier purpose/mortality and buying-time papers were considered during research but not used in the new article when direct text retrieval was incomplete. The new Happiness research paragraph instead cites the accessible original hobby study. This avoids silently carrying unverified details forward from the earlier documents.
